# Calvinism Is a Different Gospel - GOSPEL vs ED

## The Claim

[[Tim Stratton]]'s closing argument: exhaustive-divine-determinist (ED) Calvinism is not merely mistaken but a **"different gospel"** under Galatians 1:9. He states the true gospel as the acronym **GOSPEL**, then rewrites each letter as ED Calvinism entails it — and the result is "not good news... it's horrible news to the vast majority of humanity."[^1] Since the same "different gospel" test Protestants apply to Mormonism is here turned on Calvinism, "if it's good for the goose, it's good for the gander."[^2]

## Supporting Evidence

### 1. The true GOSPEL, letter by letter (Argument weight: Primary)

Stratton's acronym, each letter with its proof-text:[^3]

- **G** — **God**, a perfect/maximally great being, "created all people to be in an eternal loving relationship with him" ([[Psalm 100.3|Psalm 100:3]]).
- **O** — **Our sins** infect and separate us from God, "like oil and water" ([[Romans 3.23|Romans 3:23]]).
- **S** — **Sins** cannot be removed by our good deeds; "our good works are like filthy rags" ([[Isaiah 64.6|Isaiah 64:6]]).
- **P** — **Paying the price**: "Jesus died and rose again... Jesus paid it all" ([[Romans 5.8|Romans 5:8]]).
- **E** — **Everyone** who trusts in Christ alone "and has not freely rejected his offer of love and grace has eternal life" ([[John 3.16|John 3:16]]).
- **L** — **Life** with Jesus "starts now and lasts forever" ([[John 10.28|John 10:28]]).

### 2. The ED "gospel," letter by letter (Argument weight: Primary)

Stratton then inverts each point as exhaustive determinism requires:[^4]

- **G** — "God created some humans to be with him, but most humans to be separated from him for eternity. Right off the bat, different gospel."
- **O** — "Our separation from God is caused and determined by God."
- **S** — "Sins are illusory. No one truly misses the mark... you always do exactly what God causally determines you to do."
- **P** — "Paying the price for what God determined, Jesus died and rose again."
- **E** — "Everyone who God determines to be in hell suffers for eternity."
- **L** — "Lives damned to hell are doomed from the womb, prior to the foundations of the world."

### 3. The anathema (Argument weight: Primary — Scriptural)

Stratton lands the point on Galatians 1:9: even "an angel from heaven" preaching a different gospel is "accursed" — and he insists on applying the standard evenhandedly to his own tradition rather than only to outsiders.[^2] See [[Galatians 1.9|Galatians 1:9]]. The upshot is not that individual Calvinists are lost but that the *system*, followed to its end, "makes a mockery of... evangelism and eternity and salvation and assurance."[^5]

## Best Quotes

> "God created some humans to be with him, but most humans to be separated from him for eternity. Right off the bat — different gospel." — [[Tim Stratton]][^4]

> "It's good news for a few people, but it's horrible news to the vast majority of humanity. So it does seem to me that this is a different gospel." — [[Tim Stratton]][^1]

## Counter-Arguments / Steelmanning

### Objection 1: Calvinism preaches the same gospel, rightly understood
**Steelman (Calvinist):** The gospel is that Christ saves sinners who cannot save themselves — which Calvinism affirms *more* consistently than any rival, since it denies even a cooperative human contribution. Election is the ground of assurance, not a horror: the saved are saved by sheer grace, and God's justice in leaving others to their chosen rebellion wrongs no one. Reframing predestination as "doomed from the womb" is a caricature that ignores human guilt in Adam.

**Response:** Stratton's charge is precise: it is the *universality* of God's salvific will that the ED scheme denies (he holds 1 Timothy 2 and 2 Peter 3 teach God "desires all people" to be saved), and a "gospel" that is structurally bad news for most of humanity fails the very definition of *euangelion* (good news). The Calvinist counter that this is God's prerogative is exactly what the companion notes [[The Calvinist God Cannot Love Because He Controls Everything]] and [[Hell for Gods Glory Is Narcissism Not Love]] contest.

### Objection 2: The "different gospel" test proves too much and boomerangs on Mormonism
**Steelman:** If mere doctrinal difference about God's decree makes "another gospel," then LDS teaching — a different metaphysics of God, exaltation, degrees of glory — is at least as vulnerable to Galatians 1:9.

**Response:** This is precisely the comparison the episode leans into: Stratton argues that getting God's *character* wrong (a deceiving, most-damning deity) is a graver defect than getting his *ontology* wrong, so Calvinism fares worse on the very test Protestants press against Mormonism. See [[Getting Gods Character Wrong Is Worse Than Getting His Ontology Wrong]].
