# Mormon, Moroni, and Moronihah Encode the Palace and the Ark

## The Claim
[[Rob Kay]] reads three related Book of Mormon names as sharing a Hebrew **mem-prefix of origin/belonging** ("from / of / the place of," tied to *maqom*, "the place," a rabbinic euphemism for God) attached to a **royal-throne noun**:
- **Mormon** = *mem* + *armon* (אַרְמוֹן, "citadel / palace / high place of the king") → "the book of the palace of the king."
- **Moroni** = *mem* + *aron* (אָרוֹן, "the ark / sacred chest," the Ark of the Covenant / *Aron ha-Qodesh*) → "the place of my ark / my throne."
- **Moronihah** = *aron* + the theophoric suffix **-yah** → "the ark of Yah."

The family, he argues, marks these figures as **temple-throne** names, and Moronihah's battle in Helaman re-enacts the Ark's encirclement of Jericho. This is a *typological/etymological* reading layered on the phonetic method of [[Book of Mormon Names Are Phonetic Transliterations of Spoken Hebrew]] — suggestive, not attested. [^1]

## Supporting Evidence

### 1. Mormon as "the palace / citadel of the king," foregrounded at first mention — rate: moderate
Kay's "closest to concrete" gloss: "the book of the citadel or the palace or the high place of the king," related to Hebrew *heikhalot* (palaces) literature. He notes the *first* mention frames Mormon as a royal precinct named by the king — "a place which was called Mormon, having received its name from the king, being in the borders of the land … infested … by wild beasts" (Mosiah 18:4) — so the "place / waters / forest of Mormon" describe the whole royal territory, not just a building. [^1] [^2]

### 2. The Mosiah 18:30 hexad as a "covenantal seal" — rate: weak (gematria-style counting)
Kay counts **six** occurrences of "Mormon" in [[Mosiah 18.30|Mosiah 18:30]] ("done in Mormon … waters of Mormon … place of Mormon … waters of Mormon … forest of Mormon"), which he reads as a "Solomonic spatial consecration" — the four compass directions plus up and down, forming a sacred cube. This is the episode's clearest instance of pattern-counting and should be weighted accordingly. [^3]

### 3. Moroni as "the place of my ark / throne" — rate: moderate
*Aron* is the Ark of the Covenant, "the throne of the *Shekhinah*." So "Moroni" reads as "the place of my ark," which Kay ties to the **angel Moroni atop LDS temples**: the statue "declares … this is the place of my ark, the place of my throne." [[Don Bradley]] supplies a supporting inversion (from *The Lost 116 Pages*): the Ark was a **gold box holding stone tablets**, while the Nephite repository was a **stone box holding gold plates**. [^4] [^5]

### 4. Moronihah as "the ark of Yah" (theophoric) — rate: moderate
Adding **-yah** (short form of the Tetragrammaton) places the bearer "inside the divine name," exactly as *Yeshaʿyahu* (Isaiah), *Yirmeyahu* (Jeremiah), and *ʿOvadyah* (Obadiah) do. So Moronihah = "the ark that belongs to the divine name — his portable palace." [^6]

### 5. The "Jericho protocol" in Helaman 1 — rate: weak-moderate (thematic typology)
In [[Helaman 1.15-34|Helaman 1]], Moronihah encircles the Lamanites and the invader Coriantumr (who had driven into Zarahemla), who is "left dead" — which Kay maps onto **Joshua encircling Jericho with the Ark** ([[Coriantumr Decodes as the Hebrew Title Sar-On-Tom-Ur|Coriantumr]] being the adversary). The Ark is God's *portable war-throne* (the *merkavah*), so the episode dramatizes an Israelite rule of engagement: "only God can command us to go to war … but we have every right to defend." [^7] [^8]

## Counter-Arguments / Steelmanning
*Armon* and *aron* are genuine Hebrew words, but the objections are serious. (a) The **mem-prefix + throne-noun parse is chosen to fit** — it presupposes the very Hebrew segmentation it claims to discover, and ignores the simpler hypothesis that these are just names (Moroni is, after all, introduced with no gloss at all). (b) **Counting six "Mormon"s as a deliberate seal is textbook pattern-fitting**; the repetition is a plausible Hebraic rhetorical device but proves nothing about a hidden geometry. (c) The **Jericho parallel is thematic and portable** — any encirclement victory can be read against Joshua 6 after the fact. (d) **No ancient source attests "Mormon = citadel"** or "Moroni = ark"; these are Kay's reconstructions, not documented onomastics.

The defensible residue: the *armon* sense is at least *consonant with* the first-mention framing at Mosiah 18, which really does foreground "named by the king / borders / the place" — a royal-precinct connotation that fits better than random chance, and that dovetails with the vault's independent temple reading of the Waters of Mormon ([[Alma Baptizes by the Authority of El Shaddai at the Waters of Mormon]]). Treat this note as a *typological enrichment* of that scene, not as linguistic proof.
