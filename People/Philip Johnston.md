# Philip Johnston

Philip S. Johnston is a British evangelical Old Testament scholar associated with Ridley Hall, Cambridge, whose research centers on death, the underworld, and afterlife in the Hebrew Bible.

## Shades of Sheol
Cited by [[Alex Arnold]] on *Moroni's Standard*: in *Shades of Sheol: Death and Afterlife in the Old Testament* (2002) Johnston "documented the Hebrew underworld theology comprehensively," and his conclusions "map very closely to what [[Jon Levenson]] and others have found" — early Israelite theology had no robust *positive* afterlife but was centered on the whole person, and the resurrection hope that eventually developed "was bodily and specific, rather than spiritual and general."[^1] As a British *evangelical* (not an LDS or liberal-critical) scholar, he is a notably independent witness to the framework. See [[The Hebrew Person Is a Psychophysical Unity Not a Soul in a Body]] and [[Sheol Was the Undifferentiated Realm of All the Dead]].
