# Caroline Walker Bynum

Caroline Walker Bynum is a distinguished historian of medieval Christianity, University Professor emerita at Columbia and former faculty at the Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton, known for her work on the body, materiality, and belief in the Latin Middle Ages.

## The Medieval Resurrection Puzzles
Cited by [[Alex Arnold]] on *Moroni's Standard*: in *The Resurrection of the Body in Western Christianity* (1995) Bynum documented the theological knots that a Platonic anthropology produced over a thousand years — whether a cannibal's resurrection body includes the flesh he consumed; what happens to a drowned body eaten by fish that are eaten by other fish; whether a body rises at peak age or age at death. Arnold reads these as the fingerprints of a framework that "gave them no real coherent way to value the body as constitutive of who a person actually is."[^1] Her documentation supplies the historical symptom in [[The Immortal Soul Is a Platonic Import Not a Biblical Doctrine]] and [[LDS Resurrection Doctrine Recovers the Hebrew Whole-Person Framework]].
