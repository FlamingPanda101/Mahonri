# Jeff Harmon

Co-founder of **Angel Studios**, the media company behind faith-based film and TV, and a filmmaker. Introduced on *Ward Radio* by host [[Cardon Ellis]] as a "Catholic filmmaker apologist," though within the same episode Harmon describes keeping the Latter-day Saint **Word of Wisdom as a covenant** — teaching his children why the family abstains from coffee, tea, and alcohol, and distinguishing a covenant (a promise made with God, like the Nazarite vow of Samson) from a universal commandment.[^1]

## "All We Can Do" Is a Defined Term
Harmon's contribution to the [[2 Nephi 25.23|2 Nephi 25:23]] debate is a **"defined term"** reading borrowed from contract law. Drawing on Angel Studios' litigation experience — where a capitalized, defined term in a legal document is fixed by its own in-document definition — he argues the Book of Mormon internally defines "all we can do" at [[Alma 24.11|Alma 24:11]], where it means **to repent**. So 2 Nephi 25:23 should be read "saved by grace after we repent." He first laid this out in a Substack article (with an accompanying Grok-made meme) and presented it on Ward Radio. See [[All We Can Do Is Defined as Repentance in Alma 24]].[^2]

He also presses a pastoral point: Latter-day Saints often "jump over salvation to get to exaltation," conflating the gift of faith-and-repentance with the covenant path and burning out — see [[Conflating Salvation with Exaltation Breeds Works Burnout]].[^3]
