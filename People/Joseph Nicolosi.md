# Joseph Nicolosi

**Full Name:** Joseph Nicolosi Sr. (1947–2017)
**Tradition:** Roman Catholic
**Role:** Clinical psychologist; co-founder of NARTH; leading proponent of "reparative therapy"

## Bio
Joseph Nicolosi Sr. was an American clinical psychologist and the best-known proponent of
"reparative therapy" for male homosexuality. Working in California, he co-founded NARTH (the
National Association for Research and Therapy of Homosexuality) and popularized a
**developmental "father-wound" theory** — that male homosexuality often arises from a
deficit in the father-son bond and unmet same-sex emotional needs that become sexualized,
which therapy could supposedly "repair." His model is **rejected by the mainstream
mental-health professions** (the American Psychological Association and allied bodies) as
lacking scientific support and as carrying risk of harm; orientation-change efforts of this
kind are now widely discouraged or restricted.

## Referenced in the ingested source
[[Timber Harvard|Timber Harward]] cites "Dr. Nicolosi Sr." to *distinguish* his own
experience from the reparative-therapy template: the father-wound story "was never my
story," even though he can see how other early factors (peer conflict, isolation) may have
been sexualized for him.[^1] Nicolosi's theory thus functions in the vault as the
best-known form of the developmental account that [[Robinson's Developmental Account of
Same-Sex Attraction]] both partly resembles and explicitly distances itself from — a
distinction drawn out in that note's steelman.
