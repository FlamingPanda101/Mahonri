# Sam Shamoun

**Era:** Contemporary
**Tradition:** Christian (apologist/polemicist)
**Role:** Online apologist and debater, frequently in anti-Mormon and inter-religious polemics
**Authority:** D — combative lay polemicist; cited as opinion/polemic, not scholarship

## Bio

Sam Shamoun is a Christian apologist known for an aggressive, confrontational debate style. On the [[Ward Radio]] livestream he is discussed as a co-host (with [[Cameron Bertuzzi]] on Capturing Christianity) of a "Refuted" response stream attacking [[Jacob Hansen]]'s debate with God Logic. The panel — [[Cardon Ellis]], [[Luke Hansen]], and [[Hayden Carroll]] — characterizes his contribution as personal insult and "Gish gallop" rather than substantive argument, and references reported admissions of domestic violence and his insulting viewers in chat ("your mom is a prostitute").[^1] Because he is cited only as a hostile commentator and his remarks are presented as polemic rather than argument, he is assigned Tier D.

## Topics

### The Trinity vs Godhead "Refuted" Response
Shamoun appears as the principal antagonist in the post-debate commentary cycle the stream is reacting to, "yelling at the screen about the Old Testament" while Bertuzzi looked uncomfortable. The panel treats his "challenge" to Ward Radio as something they'd accept only if it became "a conversation," not Gish-galloping.[^2] See [[Why the Trinity vs Godhead Debate Actually Matters]], [[Calvinism Is the Logical Conclusion of Classical Theism]].

### The Las Vegas Debate and "Change of Heart"
Shamoun debated two Latter-day Saint creators — [[Latterday Logic]] (Devin) and [[Gospel Guardian]] (Braden) — on Brian Davila's channel "The Way" in Las Vegas. Contrary to his reputation for "steamrolling" opponents, he ended by publicly declaring he had "lost the debate because my heart was changed," saying he "fell in love" with the two men and now questions whether he wants to debate at all. See [[Christlike Love Softened a Hostile Debater - The Las Vegas Miracle]].[^3] During the exchange he also made several notable **concessions as a hostile witness**:
- **Monarchy of the Father.** Defining the Trinity via the *monarchia* (the Father as the one God / source of divinity, Son begotten, Spirit spirated), he granted it "sounds super similar to what we believe in terms of the godhead" and that Western Trinitarianism departs from the church fathers — folding into [[I Believe in One God the Father Almighty Encodes the Monarchy of the Father]] and [[Social and Subordinate Trinitarianism Is the Early Church View]].[^4]
- **Exaltation is more compelling.** He admitted the LDS "become like God" vision of heaven beats the Protestant "celibate bachelor forever," and retracted a caricature he'd been spreading. See [[A Trinitarian Critic Concedes LDS Exaltation Makes Heaven More Compelling]].[^5]
- **Creation ex nihilo.** He argued for *ex nihilo* from the apocryphal [[2 Maccabees 7.28|2 Maccabees 7:28]] and from [[Zechariah 12.1|Zechariah 12:1]] ("formeth the spirit of man"), answered by [[Formed the Spirit of Man Implies Pre-Existing Material Not Ex Nihilo]] and [[2 Maccabees 7.28 Does Not Teach Creation Ex Nihilo]].[^6]

**Note on trajectory:** the panel reports Shamoun **recently converted from Baptist/Protestant to Catholicism** and is "still figuring some of these things out."[^7]
