# Daniel (The Free Fidian)

**On-air name:** Daniel
**Era:** Contemporary (2020s)
**Tradition:** Protestant / Baptist (evangelical)
**Role:** Political-and-religion commentator and online content creator; LDS critic

## Bio
Daniel hosts **The Free Fidian**, a social-media channel (his bio claims 12M+ views) covering "all things you're not supposed to talk about, politics and religion," and has appeared on programs such as *Liberty Sentinel* and the *Dennis Prager Show*. A self-described Florida Baptist who leans on "speak the truth in love," he stresses that he does not subscribe to Calvinism or Arminianism but "to what the Bible says." He takes the Bible as his sole authority and does not hold denominational history (Baptist or otherwise) as authoritative.

## Debate — Are Latter-day Saints Christians? (Ward Radio)
Daniel took the **negative** against [[Devin Barnes|Devin]] (Latter-day Logic) in the [[WARD RADIO - Are LDS Really Christians Debate|Ward Radio debate]], moderated by [[Cardon Ellis]]. His case:

- **A Christian is biblically *regenerate* — and he targets the organization, not individuals.** Salvation is God replacing the old heart with a new one by grace, not works; an individual in an LDS congregation may be Christian, but the LDS *organization* is not. See [[Latter-day Saints Are Christians Just Not Creedal Christians]] and [[Four Senses of Christian and the Equivocation Charge]].[^1]
- **Test Joseph Smith; his testable claims fail.** Polygamy promised the highest exaltation; the Joseph Smith Translation inserted manuscript-less prophecies of Joseph into Genesis 50 and Isaiah 29 (absent from the Great Isaiah Scroll); the Book of Abraham papyri do not match the translation. By [[John 3.12|John 3:12]], if the earthly claims fail the heavenly ones cannot be trusted. See [[Every Testable Joseph Smith Claim Fails Only Subjective Evidence Survives]] and [[The Restoration Repeats Christianitys Own Break From Judaism]] (which he rebuts).[^2]
- **The degrees-of-glory afterlife guts evangelistic urgency.** If nearly everyone reaches a kingdom of glory, the biblical "fear of the Lord" and the two destinations of [[Matthew 25.31-46|Matthew 25]] lose their force. See [[LDS Afterlife - Degrees of Glory]].[^3]
