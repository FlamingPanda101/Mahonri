# Dan Beecher

**Role:** Podcast co-host; public educator
**Era:** Contemporary (active 2020s)
**Tradition:** Academic biblical studies (popular-level)

## Bio

Dan Beecher co-hosts the *Data Over Dogma* podcast with [[Dan McClellan]], presenting academic biblical scholarship to broad audiences in an accessible format. The podcast addresses common misconceptions about the Bible, contrasts academic and confessional approaches to scripture, and features discussions of biblical texts, history, and theology.

## Twisted Scripture: Proverbs 3:5-6
With [[Dan McClellan]], Beecher co-hosts the "twisted scripture" episode on [[Proverbs 3.5-6|Proverbs 3:5-6]], sharpening the case that the verse is a "thought-stopping cliché." Positioned as the non-believer to McClellan's believer, he presses the parity point (rival readers each claim the Spirit yet disagree) and the corollary that "even if the spirit told me something, I would still have to lean on my own understanding to know what it means." See [[Proverbs 3.5-6 as a Thought-Stopping Cliche]] and [[The Bible Has No Inherent Meaning Apart From Interpretation]]; source note [[Data Over Dogma - The Verse That Ends Every Argument]].

## Epistle of James: Faith and Works
Co-hosting the *Data Over Dogma* "chapter and verse" walkthrough of James with [[Dan McClellan]], Beecher plays the lay foil in the faith-versus-works discussion — recalling that when he first heard apologists argue faith versus works he thought "you guys are saying the exact same thing," and declaring it "the official stance of this podcast that mercy triumphs over judgment." See [[James 2 Was Written to Rebut Paul on Faith and Works]] and [[Justice and Mercy Are Not in Standing Tension]]; source note [[Data Over Dogma - Is Faith Without Works Dead]].
