# Jeff Markham

**Role:** Latter-day Saint apologist and lay scriptorian; writer-contributor to FAIR (FairMormon)
**Era:** Contemporary (active 2010s–2020s)
**Tradition:** LDS

## Bio

Jeff Markham is a lifelong Latter-day Saint who describes himself as "an amateur scriptorian" and "an intellectually curious person."[^1] Over roughly a year — reading the Book of Mormon cover to cover about every two weeks — he and a friend catalogued what became **120+ "internal consistencies"**: subtle factual details planted in one passage and only redeemed dozens of chapters later, with no contradictions. FAIR published the study in 15 parts. He stresses that the exercise is "a party trick," a gateway that got his "nose in the book," and that the real purpose of the Book of Mormon is to bring readers to Christ.[^1]

## Internal Consistency as Evidence of Authenticity

Markham's signature argument is that the density of undesigned internal consistencies — set against the documented dictation conditions (~270,000 words, ~60 working days, no notes, face in a hat, no revisions) — is unaccountable on any naturalistic theory of the Book of Mormon's origin. See:
