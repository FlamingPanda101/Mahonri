# Brad Wilcox

Latter-day Saint educator and speaker known for grace-centered teaching on the Atonement. *Distinct from the sociologist [[W. Bradford Wilcox]] and from [[Miranda Wilcox]].*

## "He Makes All the Difference"
In discussions of [[2 Nephi 25.23|2 Nephi 25:23]] and grace, Wilcox is cited on *Ward Radio* for the formulation that **"Jesus doesn't make up the difference, but he makes all the difference"** — reframing grace as the whole of salvation rather than a top-up added after human effort falls short. The hosts group this "Wilcox route" with [[Steven Robinson|Stephen Robinson]]'s bicycle parable as the familiar Latter-day Saint answer to the works-salvation charge. See [[All We Can Do Is Defined as Repentance in Alma 24]] and [[LDS Salvation Is by Grace Not Earned by Works]].[^1]
