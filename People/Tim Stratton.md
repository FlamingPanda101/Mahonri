# Tim Stratton

Tim Stratton is a Protestant philosopher-theologian, a **Molinist** (he calls himself a "Reformed theologian" who nonetheless rejects Calvinism), and the founder and public face of **Freethinking Ministries**. Raised in an Evangelical Free church in Nebraska, he became a youth pastor, earned a master's in apologetics, and completed a **PhD in theology at a Reformed university** — his dissertation arguing that one can be *Reformed* without being *Calvinist*.[^1] He is a former **five-point (TULIP) Calvinist and exhaustive divine determinist** who left the view after "preaching it as a pastor for years."[^2] He debated [[James White]] in 2022 on whether Molinism is biblical.[^3]

## Molinism and Middle Knowledge
Stratton's constructive alternative to TULIP is the **TRUMP** acronym, built on God's middle knowledge. See [[TRUMP - The Molinist Alternative to TULIP]]. His book *Human Freedom, Divine Knowledge, and Mere Molinism* argues Molinism "takes all the chips" — making sense of both the predestination and the free-will passages of Scripture.[^4]

## The Freethinking and Deity-of-Deception Arguments
His two signature arguments against determinism are the **Freethinking Argument** (a determined mind cannot rationally infer — see [[The Freethinking Argument - Determinism Undermines Rationality]], the argument his ministry is named after) and the **Deity of Deception Argument** (exhaustive divine determinism makes God a deceiver — see [[The Deity of Deception Argument Against Exhaustive Divine Determinism]]).[^5]

## Calvinism as a Different Gospel
With co-presenter Phil Colberg, Stratton gave a talk at the Evangelical Philosophical Society (Boston) applying the same test used to critique Mormonism to Calvinism — and finding "worse problems." See [[Getting Gods Character Wrong Is Worse Than Getting His Ontology Wrong]] and [[Calvinism Is a Different Gospel - GOSPEL vs ED]].[^6]
