# Brian Davila

**Role:** Christian YouTube host and content creator (*The Way with Brian Davila*)
**Era:** Contemporary (active 2020s)
**Tradition:** Protestant / non-denominational Christian (host; not the primary debater)

## Bio

Brian Davila runs the YouTube channel *The Way*, which stages cross-confessional debates and interviews between Christians of different traditions — Protestant, Catholic, Orthodox, Latter-day Saint, and others — on contested theological questions. He typically moderates and occasionally interjects rather than serving as a primary debater, and invites guests and members to appear through the channel.

## Hosting the "Who Goes to Heaven?" Debate

Davila hosted and moderated a long, heated salvation debate between the Reformed Protestant evangelist [[Ryan (Protestant Debater)|Ryan]] (visiting from Australia) and Latter-day Saint apologist [[Jacob Hansen]], framed around the question "how can we be saved?" He opens by welcoming Ryan and steps in to keep the exchange on track (e.g., pressing both to give a clear answer on the LDS "three levels of heaven"). The debate ranges over grace and works, justification, degrees of glory, baptism and the church fathers, assurance, and the reliability of scripture. See:
