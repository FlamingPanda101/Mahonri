# Ryan (Protestant Debater)

**Role:** Reformed Protestant street/online evangelist and apologist
**Era:** Contemporary (active 2020s)
**Tradition:** Reformed / Calvinist Protestant

*(Surname not stated in the source; "Ryan" is how the host and interlocutor address him. Disambiguated in the filename to avoid a bare-"Ryan" collision.)*

## Bio

Ryan is a Reformed Protestant evangelist who traveled from Australia (a "15-hour flight") to debate on [[Brian Davila]]'s channel *The Way*. He describes evangelizing "in the streets" and "online," and estimates that roughly 90% of *professing* Christians are not truly saved because they trust in their own goodness rather than in Christ alone. His theology is thoroughly monergistic and sola-fide: total depravity ("dead in trespasses and sins"), the necessity of a God-given "new heart" (Ezekiel 36), imputed righteousness (Philippians 3:9), eternal security / perseverance of the saints, and the infallibility of scripture (2 Peter 1:20-21). He debates in a rapid, cross-examining style, pressing his interlocutor to answer a single question directly and repeatedly appealing to context.

## Salvation: Sola Fide and the "Necessary and Sufficient" Faith

Against [[Jacob Hansen]], Ryan argues that trusting Christ is *both necessary and sufficient* for salvation, works being the fruit of justification and never a condition; any added law-keeping "severs" a person from Christ (Galatians 5). See:
- [[Works Are Necessary but Not Sufficient vs Faith Is Necessary and Sufficient]]
- [[Justification Is Covenant Entry Not a Guaranteed Final Verdict]]

## Assurance and Perseverance

Ryan claims 100% assurance of his own salvation, grounded not in introspection but in God's promise to keep those who are His ("I'm kept for Jesus Christ"; John 10; Romans 8; 1 Corinthians 1:8). See [[Can a True Believer Fall Away]].

## Baptism, the Church Fathers, and Heaven

He holds that baptism is a commanded but non-saving act of obedience, meeting Hansen's church-fathers reductio with Matthew 7 and the narrow-road warning; and that "heaven" is being with the triune God, not a graded set of kingdoms. See:
- [[The Church Fathers Reductio Against Sola Fide on Baptism]]
- [[Being With Heavenly Father vs a Reward in Glory]]
