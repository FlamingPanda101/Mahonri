# Michael S. Heiser (1963-2023)

Evangelical biblical scholar, Ph.D. in Hebrew Bible and Semitic languages, best known for academic work on the **divine council** in ancient Israelite religion (*The Unseen Realm*). Not a Latter-day Saint, but his work on Old Testament divine plurality is widely cited by LDS apologists.

## Divine plurality / "trial of the gods"
Cited on Ward Radio for the point that **Yahweh was the destroyer of other gods** — context that reframes [[Isaiah 43-10 - No God Formed Before Me Rebutted|Isaiah 43:10]] as a courtroom polemic against idols (Isaiah 40-48) rather than a flat denial that any other divine beings exist [^1].

## The Unseen Realm and the divine council
[[Alex Arnold]] makes Heiser his lead witness that the original Hebrew cosmos had Yahweh presiding over an assembly of divine beings (the *bene elohim*), running through Psalm 82, Job 1, Genesis 6, and the prophetic literature — documented in *The Unseen Realm* — and that this framework "was systematically suppressed in the standardization of the text," with [[Deuteronomy 32.8-9|Deuteronomy 32:8]] ("sons of God" → "sons of Israel") as the clearest single illustration. Arnold stresses Heiser was "a Protestant and no lover of LDS theology." See [[Divine Council and Deification Were Native to the Hebrew Bible]] and [[What the Deuteronomists Cut Is What the Creeds Replaced]].[^2]
