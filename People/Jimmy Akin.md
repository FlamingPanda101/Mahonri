# Jimmy Akin

Senior Catholic Answers apologist and author of *The Drama of Salvation*. (Ward Radio-style auto-captions spell it "Aken.")

## Justification
Source of two key points in the James 2 argument: "the faith isn't the problem — it's being alone that's the problem," and the **lamp analogy** (baptism gives pure righteousness; good works increase its *brightness*). See [[Justification - Infusion vs Legal Fiction]] and [[Initial vs Ongoing Justification]] [^1].

## Sola Scriptura (as a foil)
Akin is the representative Catholic in [[In Philosophers Garb - Best Argument for Sola Scriptura|In Philosopher's Garb]]'s objection section: his self-refutation syllogism (from his debate with [[James White]]) and his oral-tradition / Council-of-Jerusalem case for an ongoing magisterium are the objections the host tries to sidestep. See [[Sola Scriptura as the Bibles Veto Power Over Tradition]] and [[The Epistemic Privilege Argument for Sola Scriptura]].
