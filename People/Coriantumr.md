# Coriantumr

**Era:** Jaredite period (Book of Mormon, pre-Nephite)
**Tradition:** Book of Mormon figure (last king of the Jaredites)

## Identity
Coriantumr is the last king of the Jaredites, the sole survivor of the civil war that annihilated his people. [[Ether 15.32|Ether 15:32]] leaves him "as if he had no life" after he beheads his final rival, Shiz. He is later revealed to have survived: the people of Zarahemla (the Mulekites) discovered him and he "dwelt with them for the space of nine moons" before dying ([[Omni 1|Omni 1:21]]). The large engraved stone the people of Zarahemla brought to [[Mosiah|Mosiah I]] gave an account of Coriantumr and the destruction of his people (Omni 1:20-22).

*(Not to be confused with a later Nephite-era dissenter of Zarahemla lineage who also bears the name in Helaman 1 — the bearer whose Hebrew etymology is treated in [[Coriantumr Decodes as the Hebrew Title Sar-On-Tom-Ur]].)*

## Internal consistency across two records
Coriantumr is the hinge of one of [[Jeff Markham]]'s undesigned-coincidence examples: his ambiguous fate at the end of the Jaredite record ([[Ether 15.32|Ether 15:32]]) is quietly resolved a whole book away in [[Omni 1|Omni 1:21]], connecting the Jaredite and Nephite records through one otherwise-idle detail. See [[Coriantumr Among the People of Zarahemla Ties Ether to Omni]]. The reversed-then-forward Jaredite king-list that ends with him is discussed in [[The Dictation Feat - Reversed Jaredite Kings and a Hundred Names of Christ]].
