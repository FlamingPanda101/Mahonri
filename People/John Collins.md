# John Collins

John J. Collins is emeritus Holmes Professor of Old Testament Criticism and Interpretation at Yale Divinity School, one of the foremost scholars of apocalyptic literature and Second Temple Judaism (and author of the Hermeneia commentary on Daniel).

## Daniel's Resurrection Language as Maccabean Theodicy
Cited by [[Alex Arnold]] on *Moroni's Standard*: Collins "has argued extensively that Daniel's resurrection language arose specifically in response to" the Maccabean crisis — when the faithful were being executed under [[Antiochus IV Epiphanes]], "if justice could not be delivered in this life, it would be delivered in the next," so resurrection "emerges in the canonical text as the answer to a theodicy problem that earlier Israelite theology had not yet been forced to confront directly."[^1] See [[Bodily Resurrection Emerged as the Answer to the Maccabean Theodicy Crisis]] and [[Daniel 12]].
