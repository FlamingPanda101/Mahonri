# Gospel Guardian

**Given name (on-air):** Braden
**Era:** Contemporary (2020s)
**Tradition:** Latter-day Saint (LDS)
**Role:** Online content creator and apologist (Instagram / TikTok / YouTube as "Gospel Guardian LDS")

## Bio
Latter-day Saint content creator known online as **Gospel Guardian** (handle @gospelguardianlds). He was the "plus one" whom [[Latterday Logic]] (Devin) invited to join the Las Vegas debate against [[Sam Shamoun]] on Brian Davila's channel "The Way," aligned with Devin in study interests and communication style.

## The Las Vegas Debate with Sam Shamoun
On [[WARD RADIO - Sam Shamoun and the Las Vegas Miracle|Ward Radio]] he recounted the debate that ended with Shamoun's "change of heart." Braden proposed fasting that the conversation be productive (rather than fasting to "win"), and bought Shamoun a personalized triple combination (Book of Mormon set) as a gift. He is credited with a comparatively restrained, gracious posture in the debate. See [[Christlike Love Softened a Hostile Debater - The Las Vegas Miracle]].[^1]

He also raised the apocryphal [[Wisdom 11.17|Wisdom 11:17]] ("made the world of matter without form") as a counter to Shamoun's use of [[2 Maccabees 7.28|2 Maccabees 7:28]] for creation *ex nihilo*.[^2]
