# Josh Klene

Josh Klene is a Protestant apologist working full-time with **Freethinking Ministries** alongside [[Tim Stratton]]. The son of an Evangelical pastor, he studied at Grace University (Omaha) and Sioux Falls Seminary, and is pursuing **doctoral work on interpretive criteria** — arguing that **parsimony** and **authorial intent** decide between rival readings of a biblical passage.[^1] A former **three-point Calvinist**, he says he "always held to some form of middle knowledge" before learning the name Molinism.[^2]

## Steelman of Calvinism via Redefinition
Klene's contribution to the conversation is that Calvinism follows *only if* you accept idiosyncratic definitions of **sovereignty** (meticulous deterministic control of every molecule, including the will) and **predestination** (deterministic ordering of all things) — definitions he says "sovereignty has never carried until about ten minutes ago."[^3] See [[Predestination Does Not Entail Determinism]].

## The Assurance Problem
Klene presses the **evanescent grace** argument: the Westminster and London Baptist confessions concede that some who think they are saved are not, so Calvinism cannot ground assurance. See [[Calvinism Cannot Ground Assurance of Salvation]].[^4]

## Ontology and Salvation
He holds that correctly identifying *who* God is (ontology) matters for salvation — that one cannot have a true love relationship with "an entirely different being" than the one who exists. See [[Getting Gods Character Wrong Is Worse Than Getting His Ontology Wrong]]. He is roughly "60/40 on annihilationism" and affirms eternal conscious *separation* (not torment), citing [[C.S. Lewis]]'s *The Great Divorce*.[^5]
