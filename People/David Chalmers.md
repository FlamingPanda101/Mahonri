# David Chalmers

Australian analytic **philosopher of mind** (b. 1966), University Professor at New York University and the Australian National University and co-director of NYU's Center for Mind, Brain, and Consciousness. In *The Conscious Mind* (Oxford UP, 1996) he named and sharpened **the hard problem of consciousness** — why physical processing should be accompanied by subjective experience at all — and defended a "naturalistic dualism," using the *philosophical zombie* thought experiment to argue that experience does not logically supervene on the physical.

## Why cited
[[Alex Arnold]] enlists Chalmers as a *secular* authority whose own field concedes materialism's central gap: neuroscience maps which brain regions fire during which tasks, but "why any of that physical activity produces subjective experience at all" remains unexplained. His hard-problem framing anchors [[The Hard Problem of Consciousness Defeats Materialism]] and complements the LDS-relevant "non-conscious cannot produce conscious" argument in [[Consciousness and First Cause Problem for Atheism]].[^1]
