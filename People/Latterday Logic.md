# Latterday Logic

**Given name (on-air):** Devin
**Era:** Contemporary (2020s)
**Tradition:** Latter-day Saint (LDS)
**Role:** Online content creator and apologist (TikTok / YouTube / socials as "Latterday Logic")

## Bio
Latter-day Saint content creator known online as **Latterday Logic** (handle @latterdaylogic; site latterdaylogic.study). He was invited onto Brian Davila's channel "The Way" to debate the polemicist [[Sam Shamoun]] in Las Vegas and brought [[Gospel Guardian]] (Braden) as his "plus one." He does near-nightly TikTok live discussions on Latter-day Saint theology.

## The Las Vegas Debate with Sam Shamoun
On [[WARD RADIO - Sam Shamoun and the Las Vegas Miracle|Ward Radio]] he recounted the debate that ended with Shamoun's public "change of heart." Devin describes deliberately dropping his prepared "gotcha" traps on spiritual promptings and attributing the softened outcome to the Spirit rather than to rhetoric. See [[Christlike Love Softened a Hostile Debater - The Las Vegas Miracle]].[^1]

He deployed [[Hayden Carroll]]'s [[The Button Thought Experiment for the Problem of Hell|Creation Dilemma "button" thought experiment]] against Shamoun, who "did not have an answer," and pressed the [[Zechariah 12.1|Zechariah 12:1]] "formed the spirit" reading toward [[Formed the Spirit of Man Implies Pre-Existing Material Not Ex Nihilo|pre-existing material, not ex nihilo]].[^2]
