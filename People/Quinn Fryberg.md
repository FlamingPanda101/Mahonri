# Quinn Fryberg

**Full Name:** Quinn Fryberg
**Era:** Contemporary (2020s)
**Tradition:** Evangelical Protestant
**Role:** Christian apologist, educator, author

## Bio

Quinn Fryberg is an evangelical Christian apologist and educator. By the biographical introduction given at his debate with [[Hayden Carroll]], he has "spent the past 15 years teaching Christian apologetics in schools, churches, and college settings," is the author of "six books on apologetics and the biblical worldview," and serves as executive director of **Forging Education**, a nonprofit focused on expanding educational opportunities for families. He is a frequent speaker on apologetics, biblical worldview, and education.[^1]

## Topics

### Sola Scriptura / The Canon (Debate vs. Hayden Carroll)
In the moderated debate [[Lets Talk w Hayden Carroll - Does the Bible Teach Sola Scriptura Debate|"Does the Bible Teach Sola Scriptura?"]] Fryberg argues the **affirmative** — that the doctrine is taught in the 66-book Protestant canon. His case has four propositions: scripture is God-breathed and so authoritative, sufficient to equip the church, the standard that tests competing authorities, and unaccompanied by any continuing co-equal infallible authority.[^2] His distinctive canon epistemology is that **God determines** what is scripture while the church merely **recognizes** it under the **infallible guidance of the Holy Spirit**, so the human side of canonization is fallible but the divine side is not.[^3] Linked concepts: [[Sola Scriptura - Scripture Self-Attests Its Authority]], [[Scripture as the Test of Prophets and Revelation]], [[The Silent Years Argument Excludes the Apocrypha From the Canon]], [[The Church Recognizes Scripture It Does Not Make It]].

He answers the charge that "God led the church to the canon" is unfalsifiable by arguing that **unfalsifiability does not entail falsity** — invoking the one-way speed of light, which is unmeasurable and accepted only as a "convention," yet treated as true.[^4]
