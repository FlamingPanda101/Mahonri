# Brian Holdsworth

**Role:** Catholic YouTuber and content creator (apologetics / commentary)
**Era:** Contemporary (active 2020s)
**Tradition:** Roman Catholic

## Bio
Brian Holdsworth is a Canadian Catholic content creator known for high-production, "beautifully lit" YouTube videos defending Catholic teaching and traditionalist positions.[^1] Latter-day Saint commentators at [[Ward Radio]] note both his production polish and their long-standing irritation with his rhetorical style, and single out an earlier Holdsworth video on "vague spiritualism" as genuinely strong even while faulting his treatment of Latter-day Saints.[^1] He spoke at a [[Trent Horn]] conference (which the panel places in spring), where he referenced [[Jacob Hansen]]'s apostasy debate with [[Joe Heschmeyer]].[^2]

## The Mormon God Problem
Holdsworth's video *The Mormon God Problem* stages a "head-to-head stats comparison" between the God of classical / Nicene Christianity and the LDS Heavenly Father, arguing the Trinity is "greater" and "more consistent." Ward Radio (Cardon Ellis, Luke Hansen, Jacob Hansen, KJ Hershey) responded live, source [[WARD RADIO - My God Can Beat Up Your Mormon God]]. The panel's substantive replies:

- **Greatness equivocation.** Holdsworth's "greater" means ontological fundamentality (the uncaused cause) — a Greek presupposition Latter-day Saints reject — not moral goodness; agent-greatness is measured by the righteous use of agency. See [[Greatest Means Fundamental Not Morally Best (The Mormon God Showdown)]].
- **The impassible-God / worship problem.** His changeless, unaffectable God entails the worshiper has "zero value" to God; the LDS ground worship in freely chosen virtue and saving power. See [[An Impassible God Cannot Value the Worshiper]].
- **The presupposition critique.** Because Latter-day Saints already reject the ontological argument, comparing theisms with it is (per a Catholic reader the panel cites) "preaching to the choir." See [[Perfect-Being Theology Reads the Bible Through a Greek Lens]] and [[Mormonism Rejects Classical Theism So the God Objection Bites]].

The panel also flags his opening use of imagery from the discredited anti-Mormon film *The God Makers* as a strategic and ethical misstep,[^3] and separately criticizes his stated view that capital punishment of heretics can be justified "depending" on the time and place.[^4]
