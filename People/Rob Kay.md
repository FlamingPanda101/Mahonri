# Rob Kay

**Role:** Latter-day Saint apologist / lecturer; Jewish convert with a Hebrew background
**Era:** Contemporary (2020s)
**Tradition:** Latter-day Saint (LDS); Jewish background

## Bio
Rob Kay is a Jewish convert to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints who draws on a Hebrew (and some Greek and Hungarian — he served a mission in Hungary) linguistic background to interpret the Book of Mormon. He is a recurring guest on [[Stick of Joseph|the Stick of Joseph]] channel and gives a live lecture series in Provo (a session, promoted in this episode, on "a hidden Book of Moses in the Old Testament and the Book of Mormon"). His signature method reads Book of Mormon proper names as **Hebrew phonetic word-constructs** and then layers a **Kabbalistic / Jewish-mystical** reading (the Zohar, the sephirotic tree, gematria, *heikhalot* "palaces" literature, and *maʿaseh merkavah* "workings of the chariot") — an approach a prior Stick of Joseph episode framed via the "four levels of Hebrew interpretation" (*pardes*). *(He may be the same Robert Kay, a practicing Master Mason and endowed Latter-day Saint, featured in [[The Stick of Joseph - Real LDS Freemasons on the Endowment]]; the identification is not confirmed in the sources.)*

## Book of Mormon Onomastics (Hebrew Meanings of Names)
Kay's central apologetic thesis is that the Book of Mormon's names are not invented but heard-and-transliterated Hebrew, recoverable by the scribal "law of first mention." See:
- [[Book of Mormon Names Are Phonetic Transliterations of Spoken Hebrew]] — the governing method.
- [[Mormon Moroni and Moronihah Encode the Palace and the Ark]] — the *armon* (palace) / *aron* (ark) name family and the "Jericho protocol."
- [[Coriantumr Decodes as the Hebrew Title Sar-On-Tom-Ur]] — a "Jaredite" name read as a Hebrew title, and his "no genuine Jaredite names" contention.
- [[Ridiculed Book of Mormon Names Resolve to Hebrew Roots]] — his answer to the "made-up gibberish" charge (curelom, cumom, Shez, Lachoneus, Abinadi, Nehor).

Source: [[The Stick of Joseph - Book of Mormon Names Authenticate Ancient Origins]].
