# Testify Apologetics

**Who:** An American evangelical / Protestant apologetics YouTube channel ("Testify," self-styled by Ward Radio as "Testify Apologetics"), reported in the source at roughly 228,000–280,000 subscribers. The individual host is not named in the Ward Radio episode; the channel is treated as the critic.[^1]

**Role in this debate:** Testify published a video attempting to **debunk [[Alex OConnor|Alex O'Connor]]'s comparison** of the Book of Mormon witnesses to the resurrection witnesses. Ward Radio devotes an entire reaction episode to it, judging the arguments weak enough to call the channel "the most hilarious enemy of the church" — while conceding one point (that the witnesses left the church yet never recanted) is "a fairly strong point" for O'Connor.[^1]

## The Book of Mormon Witnesses Video
Testify's line of attack and Ward Radio's replies:
- Calls the Three Witnesses' testimony "super thin" and lacking a signed date/time/place → [[Missing Circumstantial Details Do Not Weaken the Witness Statement]].
- Argues the "faith to see metal" of [[Doctrine and Covenants 17|D&C 17]] contradicts the plates being physically hidden (the "barrel of beans" problem) → [[Seeing the Plates by Faith Means Authorization Not Invisibility]].
- Grants that the witnesses were sincere → which Ward Radio says defeats Testify's own case → [[Granting the Witnesses Sincerity Defeats the Case Against Them]].
- Runs the "we know where Jerusalem is, not Zarahemla" contrast → [[The Eyewitness Double Standard Favors the Gold Plates Over the Resurrection]].

## Other Videos Noted
The episode also references Testify videos on **Joseph Smith's motivation and plural marriage** (quoting [[Doctrine and Covenants 132]], [[Orson Pratt]], and [[Lorenzo Snow]] on the angel-with-a-drawn-sword account) and a **reply to [[Dan McClellan]]** ("How We Know the Book of Ether"). Ward Radio judged the Joseph Smith video "better" than the witnesses one because there the critic had to interact with an actual argument.[^1] Ward Radio publicly invited Testify onto the show.
