# Jon Levenson

Jon D. Levenson is a Jewish biblical scholar and the Albert A. List Professor of Jewish Studies at Harvard Divinity School, among the foremost living authorities on the Hebrew Bible and the development of Israelite religion.

## Early Israelite Afterlife and Resurrection
Cited by [[Alex Arnold]] on *Moroni's Standard*: in *Resurrection and the Restoration of Israel* (2006) Levenson documented that early Israelite religion "had no robust doctrine of individual resurrection," holding instead "a theology of national survival and covenant continuity," and that the individual's fate after death was, for most of Israel's early history, a question the tradition largely left open.[^1] His findings anchor the vault's development thesis — [[Sheol Was the Undifferentiated Realm of All the Dead]], [[Bodily Resurrection Emerged as the Answer to the Maccabean Theodicy Crisis]], and [[The Hebrew Person Is a Psychophysical Unity Not a Soul in a Body]] — and, per Arnold, he has "no institutional reason whatsoever to arrive at conclusions favorable to the LDS restoration."
