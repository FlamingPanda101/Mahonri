# Luke 17:10

> So likewise ye, when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do. *(KJV)*

## Significance
The "unprofitable servant" saying: even after complete obedience, the disciple has earned nothing and remains wholly dependent on God's mercy. [[Dan McClellan]] identifies this verse as the taproot of an 18th–19th-century devotional idiom in which "after all he can do, he is still an unprofitable servant" means *despite* everything he can do — the same idiom that governs [[2 Nephi 25.23|2 Nephi 25:23]]'s "after all we can do." The Book of Mormon quotes the saying almost verbatim in [[Mosiah 2.21|Mosiah 2:21]].
