# Deuteronomy 31:24-26

> And it came to pass, when Moses had made an end of writing the words of this law in a book, until they were finished, That Moses commanded the Levites, which bare the ark of the covenant of the LORD, saying, Take this book of the law, and put it in the side of the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, that it may be there for a witness against thee. *(KJV)*

## Note
Moses writes the covenant terms **in a book** and has it placed beside the ark "for a witness against" Israel. Key phrase: the written law is a *witness* — a fixed, generational standard the people are measured against.

## How traditions read it
- **Evangelical (Reformed).** [[Greg (Collierville Pastor)]] makes this text the Old Testament pattern of his "sola words of the covenant": God redeems, then gives written covenant-terms that stand as a witness, informing how the people live "from generation to generation." He argues the New Testament repeats the pattern — the apostolic writings are the witness-book of the new covenant. See [[Sola Words of the Covenant - Redemption Then Covenant Writings]].
- **Latter-day Saint.** [[Hayden Carroll]] does not deny that writings can be authoritative witnesses, but denies this establishes a *closed* or *self-defining* canon, or that authority rests in the text rather than in living prophets.
