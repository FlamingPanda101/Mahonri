# Matthew 6:19-21

> 19 Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth, where moth and rust doth corrupt, and where thieves break through and steal:
> 20 But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt, and where thieves do not break through nor steal:
> 21 For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.

From the Sermon on the Mount: Jesus tells disciples to store "treasures in heaven" rather than earthly wealth, "for where your treasure is, there will your heart be also." The verse anchors the LDS framing of tithing as a discipline of the *heart* — a means of not "putting money above God" — rather than a tax. (In the source, a critic sarcastically quotes the "moth... doth corrupt" line against the Church's investment fund; the reply is that the point of the verse is precisely where one's heart is set, which is the whole purpose of tithing as spiritual formation.)
