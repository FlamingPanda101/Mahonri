# Doctrine and Covenants 17

**Ref:** Doctrine and Covenants 17 (revelation to the Three Witnesses — [[Oliver Cowdery]], [[David Whitmer]], and [[Martin Harris]] — given through [[Joseph Smith]], June 1829, Fayette, New York).

## Text (key verses)

> **2** And it is by your faith that you shall obtain a view of them, even by that faith which was had by the prophets of old.

> **5** And ye shall testify that you have seen them, even as my servant Joseph Smith, Jun., has seen them; for it is by my power that he has seen them, and it is because he had faith.

## Why it matters

This is the revelation critics point to when they ask, "Do you really need *faith* to see *metal*?" — arguing that if the plates could only be seen "by faith," they were not an ordinary physical object, yet Joseph elsewhere had to *hide* and physically guard them from thieves (the "barrel of beans" problem). The section is thus the textual hinge of the witnesses debate.

The Latter-day Saint reading is that "by your faith" names the **condition of divine authorization** to be granted a view of a real, physical object — not that the plates were invisible or immaterial. On this reading v.2 and v.5 are perfectly consistent with the plates also being a tangible object that had to be protected from thieves.
