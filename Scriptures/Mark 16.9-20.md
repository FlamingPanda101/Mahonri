# Mark 16:9-20 — The Longer Ending of Mark

> **9** Now when Jesus was risen early the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene… **17** And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues… **20** And they went forth, and preached every where, the Lord working with them… (Mark 16:9-20, KJV)

## Interpretation
These twelve verses are the disputed **"longer ending" of Mark**, absent from the earliest and best Greek manuscripts (Codex Sinaiticus and Vaticanus) and widely judged by textual critics to be a later addition to a gospel that originally ended at Mark 16:8. On [[Ward Radio]], [[Jonah Barnes]] cites it (loosely, as "13 verses added to the end of Mark") to press a consistency point: critics who dismiss the whole Gospel of Thomas over one late-looking saying keep this passage in their own Bibles — "they don't rip that out."[^1] The double standard, he argues, is the real objection to Thomas, not the corruption itself. See [[The Make Her a Man Saying Is a Late Insertion Into Thomas]].
