# 2 Chronicles 6:6

> But I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name might be there; and have chosen David to be over my people Israel. *(KJV)*

## Note
Part of Solomon's temple dedication. God declares He has **chosen Jerusalem** as the place for His name (His sanctuary) and David's line to rule. Cited in debate as evidence that the legitimate temple is fixed at Zion/Jerusalem.

## How traditions read it
- **Evangelical (Reformed).** [[Greg (Collierville Pastor)]] uses this to argue that a legitimate temple must be at God's chosen "holy hill" in Zion, so the Book of Mormon's Nephite temple in the Americas is "an unauthorized temple." See [[Nephite Temple and Priesthood Outside the Levitical Line]].
- **Latter-day Saint.** [[Hayden Carroll]] replies that God can authorize a temple outside those "Israelite confines" by direct revelation (Nephi's vision), so the choice of Jerusalem describes the covenant order of that time rather than an absolute, permanent restriction.
