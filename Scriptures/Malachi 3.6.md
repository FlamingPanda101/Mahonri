# Malachi 3:6

> For I am the LORD, I change not; therefore ye sons of Jacob are not consumed. *(KJV)*

## Note
A classic proof-text for **divine immutability** — God's unchanging nature is the ground of His covenant faithfulness ("therefore" the people are not consumed).

## How traditions read it
- **Evangelical (Reformed).** [[Greg (Collierville Pastor)]] appeals to this (paraphrasing "from everlasting to everlasting I am God... I the Lord do not change") to argue God is a "steadfast, immovable rock" who could never fall or sin — so an embodied God who "can cease to be God" is "scary and depressing," not the biblical God. See [[God Can Sin But Freely Chooses Not To (LDS Free-Will Theism)]] and the existing [[Divine Immutability - God Cannot Change]].
- **Latter-day Saint.** [[Hayden Carroll]] reads immutability as reliability of *character and covenant* rather than metaphysical changelessness of substance, consistent with a God who progressed to exaltation; the verse concerns God's steadfast covenant loyalty, not classical impassibility.
