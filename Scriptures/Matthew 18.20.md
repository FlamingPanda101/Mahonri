# Matthew 18:20 — Where Two or Three Are Gathered

> **20** For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them. (Matthew 18:20, KJV)

## Interpretation
On [[Ward Radio]], [[Jonah Barnes]] pairs this verse with a Gospel of Thomas saying — "Where there are gods, they are gods; where there are two or one, I am with them" — arguing the Thomas form is "a different version of that," but one that explicitly names the gathered disciples *gods* where Matthew leaves the status implicit.[^1] The canonical verse is usually read as a promise of Christ's presence to the assembled church; the LDS-apologetic use reads the Thomas parallel back into it as latent divinization language. See [[Gospel of Thomas Calls Believers Gods]].
