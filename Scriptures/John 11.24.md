# John 11:24

> Martha saith unto him, I know that he shall rise again in the resurrection at the last day. *(KJV)*

## Significance
Martha's reply to Jesus after Lazarus dies is a window onto **first-century Jewish hope**. Notice what she does *not* say — that Lazarus is now in heaven forever. Her confidence is in the bodily **resurrection at the last day**, when God renews creation and vindicates his covenant people. [[Jacob Hansen]] uses this verse to show that the first-century soteriological question was not the modern Protestant "how do I get to heaven when I die?" but "will God vindicate his covenant people?" — a hope of resurrection into God's everlasting kingdom on a renewed earth, not a disembodied afterlife. The same hope anchors Paul's gospel in [[1 Corinthians 15.1-4|1 Corinthians 15]].
