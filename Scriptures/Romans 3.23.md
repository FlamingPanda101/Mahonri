# Romans 3:23

> "For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;" (KJV)

## Significance
The **O** ("Our sins") of [[Tim Stratton]]'s GOSPEL acronym: "our sins infect us and separate us from God. Like oil and water — necessary perfection and infection do not mix."[^1] The verse's universality ("all have sinned") is common ground with Calvinism; the dispute is over what follows. In the inverted "gospel of ED," Stratton says, "sins are illusory — no one truly misses the mark... you always do exactly what God causally determines you to do," which he takes to empty this verse of meaning. See [[Calvinism Is a Different Gospel - GOSPEL vs ED]].

## How traditions read it
- **All traditions:** a foundational statement of universal human sinfulness.
- **Calvinist:** paired with total depravity — the "all" underwrites the need for unconditional, monergistic grace.
- **Molinist / LDS (in this source):** universal sinfulness is real *missing of the mark*, which presupposes a mark one genuinely could have hit — undercutting the determinist reading in which the "sin" was itself decreed.
