# Ether 9:19 — cureloms and cumoms among useful beasts

> And they also had horses, and asses, and there were elephants and cureloms and cumoms; all of which were useful unto man, and more especially the elephants and cureloms and cumoms. *(Ether 9:19, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Terms
- **Curelom** — an untranslated Jaredite animal name, grouped here with elephants as a "useful" beast. [[Rob Kay]] proposes it renders Hebrew *shor* (שׁוֹר), "ox / bovine — the Torah's quintessential useful beast."
- **Cumom** — likewise untranslated; Kay ties it to the root *shamem* ("large / awesome"), a big beast of burden. Both are classic "made-up gibberish" targets for critics; the note below argues they resolve to Hebrew roots (a suggestive, not probative, reading).
