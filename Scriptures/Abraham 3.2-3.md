# Abraham 3:2-3

From the [[Book of Abraham]] (LDS [[Pearl of Great Price]]), the astronomical vision given to Abraham. These verses introduce **Kolob**, the governing star "nearest unto the throne of God," and the graded order of "governing ones" that frames the book's cosmology.

> And I saw the stars, that they were very great, and that one of them was nearest unto the throne of God; and there were many great ones which were near unto it;
>
> And the Lord said unto me: These are the governing ones; and the name of the great one is Kolob, because it is near unto me, for I am the Lord thy God: I have set this one to govern all those which belong to the same order as that upon which thou standest. *(Book of Abraham)*

## Why it matters
The passage supplies the astronomical register Joseph Smith claimed for [[Facsimile 2]] — the sun, the stars, and the governing planet Kolob — so that the illustration and the text share one cosmology. Abraham 3 then moves from this astronomy into a premortal council, the selection scene treated separately in [[Abraham 3 Is the Selection of Adam Not the Redeemer]].
