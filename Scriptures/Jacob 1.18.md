# Jacob 1:18

> For I, Jacob, and my brother Joseph had been consecrated priests and teachers of this people, by the hand of Nephi. *(Jacob 1:18, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Detail
[[Jacob (Book of Mormon)|Jacob]] records that he and his brother Joseph held the office of priest and teacher, consecrated "by the hand of [[Nephi]]." The detail matches, from the other record's side, [[2 Nephi 5.26|2 Nephi 5:26]], where Nephi records performing exactly that consecration — an undesigned agreement of office, persons, and consecrator across two books.
