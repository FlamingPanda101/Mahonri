# Alma 27:17

> Now the joy of Ammon was so great even that he was full; yea, he was swallowed up in the joy of his God, even to the exhausting of his strength; and he fell **again** to the earth.

## The internal-consistency detail
The word **"again"** is the hook. Ammon's joyful collapse at reuniting with the sons of Mosiah after their missions only makes sense as a *second* falling — the first, in terror before the rebuking angel, is recorded ~20 chapters earlier in [[Mosiah 27.11-13|Mosiah 27:11-13]]. The two events invert one another (terror → joy) and bookend Ammon's conversion, yet Alma 27 gives no cue that "again" reaches back that far. Jeff Markham calls this his signature example, the one that "got me" on a re-listen ("he fell again to the earth… *again*").

## Interpretation
Latter-day Saint readers additionally read falling to the earth and the angel's command to "arise" as covenant/temple symbolism (death and rising). All traditions that treat the Book of Mormon as narrative agree the callback is internally coherent; the dispute is only over whether an unaided dictator could have planted it.
