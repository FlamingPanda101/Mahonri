# Genesis 3:15

> And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel. *(KJV)*

## Note
Traditionally called the **protoevangelium** ("first gospel") — the earliest messianic promise, given immediately after the Fall. Christian interpreters read "the seed of the woman" who crushes the serpent's head as the coming Messiah/Christ.

## How traditions read it
- **Evangelical (Reformed).** [[Greg (Collierville Pastor)]] treats this verse as the hinge of the whole biblical "story": God promises a redeemer at the moment of the Fall, and everything from Genesis to Revelation traces the coming and triumph of that promised seed. He uses it to argue salvation has always been by faith in the Messianic promise, and that the canon is the unfolding of this one story. See [[Sola Words of the Covenant - Redemption Then Covenant Writings]].
- **Latter-day Saint.** Broadly shares the messianic reading; the dispute in the debate is not over this verse but over whether the "story" it opens *closes* the canon (Greg) or remains open to continuing revelation (Carroll).
