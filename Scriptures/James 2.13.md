# James 2:13

> For he shall have judgment without mercy, that hath shewed no mercy; and mercy rejoiceth against judgment. *(KJV)*

## Significance
"Mercy rejoiceth against judgment" — rendered "mercy triumphs over judgment" in modern translations. Read by [[Dan McClellan]] as evidence that biblical justice (Hebrew *mishpat*/*tsedeqah*) is about restored right-relationship, not prescribed punishment, so mercy is not the suspension of justice but its fulfillment. Sits at the head of the [[James 2.14-26|James 2]] faith-and-works section.
