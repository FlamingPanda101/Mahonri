# Acts 7:38

> This is he, that was in the church in the wilderness with the angel which spake to him in the mount Sina, and with our fathers: who received the lively oracles to give unto us: *(KJV)*

## Note
In Stephen's speech, Moses receives the "**lively oracles**" (Greek *logia zonta*, "living oracles") to give to Israel. The phrase "living oracles" is contested between traditions because it is echoed by both a written-word reading and a living-prophet reading.

## How traditions read it
- **Evangelical (Reformed).** [[Greg (Collierville Pastor)]] argues that here "living oracles" refers to the **writings** Moses received (the covenant law), not to a living prophet — and that scripture never uses "living oracles" of a *person*. He treats this as decisive against the Latter-day Saint appeal to a living prophet as the "living oracle." See [[Sola Words of the Covenant - Redemption Then Covenant Writings]].
- **Latter-day Saint.** LDS usage often applies "living oracles" to living prophets/apostles (see [[The Living Prophet Versus the Standard Works Are Two Competing LDS Authority Traditions]] and [[Brigham Young Failed Prophet Argument]]). [[Hayden Carroll]] holds authority is located in the prophets God speaks through, not merely in the text.
