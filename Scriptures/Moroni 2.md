# Moroni 2 — The Words Christ Spoke Privately to the Twelve

> **1** The words of Christ, which he spake unto his disciples, the twelve whom he had chosen, as he laid his hands upon them— **2** And he called them by name, saying: Ye shall call on the Father in my name, in mighty prayer; and after ye have done this ye shall have power that to him upon whom ye shall lay your hands, ye shall give the Holy Ghost… **3** Now Christ spake these words unto them at the time of his first appearing; and **the multitude heard it not, but the disciples heard it**… (Moroni 2:1-3, Book of Mormon)

## Interpretation
On [[Ward Radio]], [[Jonah Barnes]] likens the opening line of the Gospel of Thomas — "these are the secret / hidden words which the living Jesus spoke, which Didymus Judas Thomas wrote down" — to this chapter, where the risen Christ gives instruction to the chosen twelve that "the multitude heard it not."[^1] The parallel: a resurrected ("living") Christ delivering privately transmitted sayings to named disciples is not a peculiarly heretical conceit — the Book of Mormon records the same pattern. Barnes's aside: "if you think that's weird, we have that line in the Book of Mormon actually… It's in Moroni chapter 2, so chill out." See [[The Gospel of Thomas Has an Early Sayings Core]].
