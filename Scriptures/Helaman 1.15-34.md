# Helaman 1:15-34 — Coriantumr's invasion and Moronihah's defense

> ...this Coriantumr...was a dissenter from among the Nephites; and he was a large and a mighty man. Therefore, the king of the Lamanites, whose name was Tubaloth...supposing that Coriantumr, being a mighty man, could stand against the Nephites, with his strength and also with his great wisdom...did appoint him to be a leader...*(Helaman 1:15-16, Book of Mormon; Coriantumr storms Zarahemla and slays Pacumeni, vv.18-21, then is himself surrounded and slain, vv.31-32)*

## Key Terms
- **Coriantumr** — a Jaredite-language name here borne by a Nephite dissenter, "a large and a mighty man" who leads the Lamanite army, seizes Zarahemla, and is killed when Moronihah's forces surround him. [[Rob Kay]] reads it as the Hebrew title *Sar-On-Tom-Ur* ("mighty / strength / complete / wisdom"), noting the description's "strength and also...great wisdom" mirrors the name.
- **Moronihah** — the Nephite chief captain who **encircles** the invaders and retakes Zarahemla, which Kay maps onto Joshua encircling Jericho with the Ark (reading Moronihah as "the ark of Yah").
