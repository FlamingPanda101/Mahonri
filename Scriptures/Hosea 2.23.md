# Hosea 2:23

> And I will sow her unto me in the earth; and I will have mercy upon her that had not obtained mercy; and I will say to them which were not my people, Thou art my people; and they shall say, Thou art my God. *(KJV)*

## Key phrase
"**Thou art my people**" — God's promise to reclaim and include those who "were not my people," extending covenant belonging beyond the previously excluded. Paul cites this verse in Romans 9:25 for the ingathering of the Gentiles.
