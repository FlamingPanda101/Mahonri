# Matthew 6:25-34 — Take No Thought / Consider the Lilies

> **25** Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on… **26** Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap… yet your heavenly Father feedeth them… **34** Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. (Matthew 6:25-34, KJV)

## Interpretation
This "do not be anxious about food and body" passage — with its Lukan parallel at Luke 12:22-31 (the ravens) — is the Synoptic material that the papyrus fragment **P.Oxy 5575** interweaves with the Gospel of Thomas.[^1] On [[Ward Radio]], [[Jonah Barnes]] notes that scholars recovered the word "worry"/"what you will eat" from the fragment, matched it here, and found the text running Thomas → Luke 12 / Matthew 6 → Thomas, even using "a different word for birds" than Matthew or Luke.[^1] See [[P.Oxy 5575 Interweaves Thomas With Matthew and Luke]].
