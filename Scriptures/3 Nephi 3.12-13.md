# 3 Nephi 3:12-13 — Lachoneus gathers the people to flee the robbers

> Now behold, this Lachoneus, the governor, was a just man, and could not be frightened by the demands and the threatenings of a robber; therefore he did not hearken to the epistle of Giddianhi, the governor of the robbers, but he did cause that his people should cry unto the Lord for strength against the time that the robbers should come down against them. Yea, he sent a proclamation among all the people, that they should gather together their women, and their children, their flocks and their herds, and all their substance, save it were their land, unto one place. *(3 Nephi 3:12-13, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Term
- **Lachoneus** — a name that looks Greek but which [[Rob Kay]] derives from a Hebrew root "to leave / cross over / flee" (cf. *Lech-Lecha*, Abraham's departure) plus *nus*, "to escape." The gloss matches his one decisive act here: he decrees that the people **gather in and flee** the Gadianton robbers — "cross over, get up and get out."
