# John 3:18

> He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God. *(KJV)*

A verse that makes **belief in the Son** the single hinge between condemnation and acquittal: the unbeliever is "condemned already," so no additional work is named as the ground of the verdict.

## In the salvation debate
[[Ryan (Protestant Debater)|Ryan]] (Reformed) quotes John 3:18 as his warrant for holding the doctrine firm while refusing to judge any individual's heart: "The Lord knows a person's heart... My mind is held captive to the word of God... 'Whoever believes in the son is not condemned, but whoever does not believe is condemned already.'" On his faith-alone reading, trust in Christ alone is what removes condemnation, so adding baptism or works as a *condition* leaves one "condemned already." [[Jacob Hansen]] replies that Ryan's system then damns the historic church (see [[The Church Fathers Reductio Against Sola Fide on Baptism]]) and makes God the one who decides who is even able to believe (see [[Regeneration Precedes Faith So We Only Seem to Choose God]]).
