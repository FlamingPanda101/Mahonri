# Mosiah 18:30 — the place, waters, and forest of Mormon

> And now it came to pass that all this was done in Mormon, yea, by the waters of Mormon, in the forest that was near the waters of Mormon; yea, the place of Mormon, the waters of Mormon, the forest of Mormon, how beautiful are they to the eyes of them who there came to the knowledge of their Redeemer; yea, and how blessed are they, for they shall sing to his praise forever. *(Mosiah 18:30, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Term
- **Mormon** — the name of the place, waters, and forest where Alma baptizes. The land was "called Mormon, having received its name from the king" (Mosiah 18:4). [[Rob Kay]] reads *Mormon* as *mem* (prefix of origin/belonging) + *armon* (אַרְמוֹן, "citadel / palace of the king"), and counts **six** occurrences of "Mormon" in this verse as a "covenantal seal" / "Solomonic spatial consecration" (the four compass points plus up and down). A pattern-counting reading — see the note below for the steelman.
