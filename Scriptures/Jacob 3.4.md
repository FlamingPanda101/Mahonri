# Jacob 3:4

> And the time speedily cometh, that except ye repent they shall possess the land of your inheritance, and the Lord God will lead away the righteous out from among you. *(Jacob 3:4, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Detail
An obscure conditional prophecy of [[Jacob (Book of Mormon)|Jacob]]: unless the Nephites repent, their enemies will possess "the land of your inheritance" and the Lord will "lead away the righteous out from among you." It carries no immediate narrative payoff — but it is redeemed a whole book later in [[Omni 1|Omni 1:12-13]] (when [[Mosiah|Mosiah I]], warned of the Lord, leads the righteous out of the land of Nephi) and confirmed in [[Mosiah 9.1|Mosiah 9:1]] (the Lamanites holding "our fathers' first inheritance").
