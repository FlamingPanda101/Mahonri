# Psalm 100:3

> "Know ye that the LORD he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture." (KJV)

## Significance
The **G** of [[Tim Stratton]]'s GOSPEL acronym. Stratton opens his statement of the true gospel with "God, a perfect being — a maximally great being — created all people to be in an eternal loving relationship with him," which he calls "the objective purpose of life and why humanity exists," and anchors it here.[^1] The verse frames existence as a relationship God initiates ("it is he that hath made us"), which for Stratton sets up the contrast with the ED-Calvinist "gospel," where God instead "created some humans to be with him, but most... to be separated from him for eternity." See [[Calvinism Is a Different Gospel - GOSPEL vs ED]].

## How traditions read it
- **Molinist / non-Calvinist (Stratton):** God's making and owning of *all people* expresses a universal salvific intent — he made everyone *for* relationship with him.
- **Calvinist:** "His people" and "the sheep of his pasture" denote the covenant elect specifically, not humanity indiscriminately; the making is creation, the belonging is election.
