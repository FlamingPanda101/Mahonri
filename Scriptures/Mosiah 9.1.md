# Mosiah 9:1

> I, Zeniff, having been taught in all the language of the Nephites, and having had a knowledge of the land of Nephi, or of the land of our fathers' first inheritance, and having been sent as a spy among the Lamanites that I might spy out their forces… *(Mosiah 9:1, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Detail
[[Zeniff]] opens his record by identifying the land of Nephi as "the land of our fathers' first inheritance" — now among the Lamanites, whom he is sent to spy on. The phrase confirms the fulfillment of [[Jacob 3.4|Jacob 3:4]]'s prophecy that the enemies "shall possess the land of your inheritance," the departure of the righteous having been recorded in [[Omni 1|Omni 1:12-13]]. The land the Nephites first settled had indeed passed to the Lamanites.
