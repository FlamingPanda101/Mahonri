# 1 John 5:11-12

> **11** And this is the record, that God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.
> **12** He that hath the Son hath life; and he that hath not the Son of God hath not life.

**[[Mark (Ex-Mormon Baptist)]]** reads these verses alongside [[John 6.47|John 6:47]] as a second present-tense proof that eternal life is a possession held now: "He that *hath* the Son *hath* life." He is careful to spiritualize the "having": "It's not something that you put in your pocket... It is within the soul... The moment that you believe, Jesus Christ enters your soul and he gives you eternal life immediately."[^1] He earlier cites 1 John 5:20 to define that eternal life as *knowing* the Son.

**[[Hayden Carroll]] agrees** with the verse's plain sense — "I believe that actually... And that's what I've been taught as a Latter-day Saint" — which he uses to argue that a present, relational possession of eternal life is *not* foreign to Restoration teaching.[^2]
