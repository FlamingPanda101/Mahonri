# John 4.24

> God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth.
> — KJV

## Immediate context & the LDS reading
The verse sits in Jesus's exchange with the Samaritan woman about *how/where* to worship. Verse 23 — "the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth" — frames "God is a Spirit" as grounding *inward* worship, not as a metaphysical denial that the Father has a body. The Greek predicate (*pneuma ho theos*, with no indefinite article) is read as qualitative — "God is spirit" like "God is love / light" — rather than an equation of God with immateriality.
