# 1 Corinthians 9:27

> But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway. *(KJV)*

## Used in
- [[Can a True Believer Fall Away]] — Hansen presses that Paul feared becoming *adokimos* ("castaway/disqualified"), the same word used elsewhere for being rejected by God, so even Paul's final salvation was not treated as a settled certainty. Ryan replies the context is Paul's *ministry* — disciplining himself so his preaching is not disqualified — not a fear of losing salvation.

## How traditions read it
- **Latter-day Saint / Arminian (Hansen):** If the apostle guards against being *adokimos*, ordinary believers cannot claim absolute, unconditional assurance.
- **Reformed / Protestant (Ryan):** The athletic metaphor concerns effective, credible ministry ("I am made all things to all men, that I might by all means save some," vv. 20-22); Paul's self-discipline protects his witness, and elsewhere he is fully confident of his own salvation (2 Timothy 1:12; Philippians 1:6).
