# Hebrews 10:26-27

> For if we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins, But a certain fearful looking for of judgment and fiery indignation, which shall devour the adversaries. *(KJV)*

## Used in
- [[Can a True Believer Fall Away]] — Hansen cites it to argue believers who "go on sinning deliberately" and stop abiding lose their sacrifice; Ryan answers from the chapter's context that the "sin" in view is the specific apostasy of rejecting Christ to return to animal sacrifices, and the passage's warnings are the very means God uses to keep the elect persevering ("let us hold fast," v.23).

## How traditions read it
- **Latter-day Saint / Arminian (Hansen):** Addressed to believers ("we"); a genuine possibility of forfeiting Christ's sacrifice by deliberate, ongoing sin — perseverance is not automatic.
- **Reformed / Protestant (Ryan):** The wilful sin is decisive apostasy (trampling the Son, profaning the blood) — a repudiation of Christ altogether; the warning functions pastorally to preserve true believers, who by definition do not finally commit it.
