# John 17:17

> Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth. *(KJV)*

## Key Greek terms
**ὁ λόγος ὁ σὸς ἀλήθειά ἐστιν (ho logos ho sos alētheia estin)** — "thy word is truth." Spoken by Jesus in the high-priestly prayer of John 17. The referent of λόγος (*logos*, "word") is God's communicated truth/message; the verse does not equate that word with the written scriptures.

## In context
A prooftext appealed to by inerrantists to identify scripture with "the word of God." [[Dan McClellan]] replies that Jesus "does not say that that word is the scriptures" — "thy word is truth" affirms the truthfulness of God's *message*, not that a particular collection of writings *is* God's word. This parallels his reading of [[John 10.33-35|John 10:35]] ("scripture cannot be broken") as asserting reliability, not identity with God's word.
