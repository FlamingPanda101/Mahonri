# John 3:12

> If I have told you earthly things, and ye believe not, how shall ye believe, if I tell you of heavenly things? *(KJV)*

Part of Jesus's night discourse to [[Nicodemus]] (John 3). The logic is *a fortiori*: if a hearer will not credit the plainer, testable ("earthly") things, he has no basis to credit the harder, unseen ("heavenly") ones.

## Used in
In the [[WARD RADIO - Are LDS Really Christians Debate|Ward Radio debate]], **Daniel** (the [[Daniel (The Free Fidian)|Free Fidian]]) makes this verse his closing hook against the LDS case: because he judges Joseph Smith's *testable* claims (polygamy-for-exaltation, manuscript-less prophecy, the Book of Abraham) to fail, John 3:12's rule means the *untestable* heavenly claims cannot be trusted either.
