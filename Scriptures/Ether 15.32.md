# Ether 15:32

> And it came to pass that Coriantumr fell to the earth, and became as if he had no life. *(Ether 15:32, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Detail
The near-final verse of the Jaredite record: after the mutual destruction of his people and beheading Shiz, [[Coriantumr]] — the last Jaredite king — "became as if he had no life." Read alone, the verse leaves his fate ambiguous (did he die?). The question is answered a whole record away in [[Omni 1|Omni 1:21]], where the people of Zarahemla discover a surviving Coriantumr who lived among them nine moons — an undesigned cross-record consistency.
