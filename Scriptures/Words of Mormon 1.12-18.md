# Words of Mormon 1:12-18

> And it came to pass also that the armies of the Lamanites came down out of the land of Nephi, to battle against his people. But behold, king Benjamin gathered together his armies, and he did stand against them; and he did fight with the strength of his own arm, with the sword of Laban… Wherefore, with the help of these, king Benjamin, by laboring with all the might of his body and the faculty of his whole soul, and also the prophets, did once more establish peace in the land. *(Words of Mormon 1:13, 18, abridged, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Detail
[[Mormon]], inserting his own words at the seam between the small plates and his abridgment, records a period of Lamanite wars in [[King Benjamin]]'s reign — Benjamin fighting "with the sword of Laban," driving the Lamanites out, and, "also [with] the prophets," establishing peace. This conflict-then-peace is silently presupposed by the opening line of the next book, [[Mosiah 1|Mosiah 1:1]]: "there was no more contention in all the land of Zarahemla… king Benjamin had continual peace all the remainder of his days."
