# Philippians 3:9

> And be found in him, not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith: *(KJV)*

## Used in
- [[Justification Is Covenant Entry Not a Guaranteed Final Verdict]] — Ryan's key text for *imputed* righteousness: at the judgment God "sees me through the lens of Christ," a righteousness "not my own" but Christ's, received by faith.
- [[Works Are Necessary but Not Sufficient vs Faith Is Necessary and Sufficient]] — cited to argue the believer stands before God "not because of my works and my deeds, but because of what Christ did alone."

## How traditions read it
- **Reformed / Protestant (Ryan):** An alien, imputed righteousness — Christ kept the law perfectly "on my behalf," and that righteousness is credited to the believer, so final acceptance rests on Christ's merit, not the believer's obedience.
- **Latter-day Saint (Hansen):** Read in the chapter's context — Paul counts his own Pharisaic achievements "as loss" to *gain* Christ and enter the covenant; the phrase describes coming to God empty-handed to begin the relationship, not a legal fiction that removes the final judgment by works.
