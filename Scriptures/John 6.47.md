# John 6:47

> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me hath everlasting life.

The single most load-bearing verse in the ex-Mormon guest **[[Mark (Ex-Mormon Baptist)]]**'s case against Latter-day Saint soteriology. He fixes on the **present-tense verb "hath"**: "*Hath* is a present tense word. Obviously, it means *has*, to possess, to hold in your hands."[^1] From this he argues eternal life is a possession one *already holds* upon belief — not, as he says LDS theology teaches, a future exaltation obtained "only after the final resurrection." Jesus pairs it seven verses earlier with John 6:40 ("this is the will of him that sent me, that every one which seeth the Son, and believeth on him, may have everlasting life").

**LDS reading (Hayden Carroll):** the verse is fully at home in Restoration scripture — [[D&C 45.3-5|D&C 45:3-5]] likewise promises those "that believe on my name... everlasting life" — and "eternal life" carries more than one sense; in the John 6 / [[1 John 5.11-12|1 John 5:11-12]] sense of *knowing and having the Son*, a believer can have it now.[^2]
