# 2 Nephi 5:26

> And it came to pass that I, Nephi, did consecrate Jacob and Joseph, that they should be priests and teachers over the land of my people. *(2 Nephi 5:26, Book of Mormon)*

## Key Detail
[[Nephi]] records consecrating his younger brothers [[Jacob (Book of Mormon)|Jacob]] and Joseph as priests and teachers. The same consecration is referred to from Jacob's side in [[Jacob 1.18|Jacob 1:18]] ("consecrated priests and teachers of this people, by the hand of Nephi") — the two verses agree on office, persons, and consecrator without either flagging the other. (Adjacent verse [[2 Nephi 5.27|2 Nephi 5:27]], "we lived after the manner of happiness," figures in a separate internal-consistency argument.)
