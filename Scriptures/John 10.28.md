# John 10:28

> "And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand." (KJV)

## Significance
The **L** ("Life with Jesus starts now and lasts forever") of [[Tim Stratton]]'s GOSPEL acronym — "as Buzz Lightyear would say, to infinity and beyond."[^1] Notably, Stratton (a former Calvinist) repurposes a verse Calvinists lean on for **eternal security / perseverance of the saints** and folds it into a gospel that also affirms resistible grace, matching the **P** of his [[TRUMP - The Molinist Alternative to TULIP|TRUMP]] acronym: the redeemed persevere freely, "too smart to sin," never *plucked out* yet never coerced. See [[Calvinism Is a Different Gospel - GOSPEL vs ED]].

## How traditions read it
- **Calvinist:** a cornerstone of eternal security — the elect *cannot* finally fall away, since none can pluck them from the Father's hand.
- **Molinist / Arminian (Stratton):** the security is real for those in a true love relationship with Christ, but it secures them *against external* loss ("no man"), not against their own free self-removal — the "great divorce" a person may still choose. The verse promises the *durability* of the gift, not the impossibility of freely rejecting it.
