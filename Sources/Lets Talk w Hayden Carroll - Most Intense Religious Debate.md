# Let's Talk w/ Hayden Carroll — The Most Intense Religious Debate You've Ever Seen

A long, wide-ranging in-person debate between **[[Greg (Collierville Pastor)]]**, a Reformed evangelical pastor from Collierville, Tennessee, and **[[Hayden Carroll]]**, a Latter-day Saint apologist and the channel host. The stated topic — *"what is the message of salvation?"* — quickly branches into the nature of God, hell, Calvinism and the problem of evil, Christology (Trinity vs. Godhead), and finally an extended clash over **sola scriptura** and the biblical canon. Both men are cordial ("I love you," Halo and Lord of the Rings tangents) but press hard.

## Summary
- **Salvation.** Greg preaches classic evangelical *sola fide* — saved by grace through faith alone, born again, "nothing you have to do to earn it."[^1] Carroll agrees salvation is by grace through faith but defines faith (Greek *pistis*) as **allegiance** (citing Matthew Bates), manifested in works and covenant obedience like baptism — though "it's not the baptism that saves us."[^2] See [[Faith Is Allegiance to Christ Not Mere Mental Assent]].
- **Nature of God and hell.** Carroll rejects "perfect-being theology... that came out of the Neoplatonism," holding God is an embodied, exalted person ("God is a human"), that matter is eternal (no creation *ex nihilo*), and that God — having **free will** — *can* sin but chooses not to, and can even "cease to be God."[^3] Greg counters that God is immaterial spirit, morally perfect from everlasting, the unchanging "steadfast rock" (Malachi), and *cannot* sin by nature. See [[God Can Sin But Freely Chooses Not To (LDS Free-Will Theism)]] and the LDS embodiment/[[Deuteronomy 32 Dead Sea Scrolls Monolatry|divine-council]] material.
- **Calvinism and the problem of evil.** Greg is a **compatibilist** ("no atom is out of place," R.C. Sproul) who says God ordains suffering for good (his 7-year infertility; a rape-conceived child; Joseph in Egypt; a Tolkien "transcendent author" analogy). Carroll's sharpest rejoinder: if God unconditionally regenerates the elect, then Greg's own view makes the debate *meaningless* — "why didn't you regenerate me?" — and turns God into "an evil God." See [[The Regeneration Rejoinder - Calvinist Evangelism Is Self-Defeating]].
- **Sola scriptura and canon.** Greg advances his coined **"sola words of the covenant"**: God redeems, then gives covenant-writings that stand as a *witness* (Exodus 20, Deuteronomy 31, Josiah's reform), so the canon "tells a story" from Genesis to Revelation and closes there — and "living oracles" (Acts 7:38) names the *writings*, not a living prophet.[^4] Carroll answers that Protestants **cannot justify their canon** (R.C. Sproul's "fallible list of infallible books"), that it was received by tradition, and that "God still speaks" through living prophets. See [[Sola Words of the Covenant - Redemption Then Covenant Writings]].
- **Book of Mormon.** Greg objects that the Nephites — descendants of Joseph, not Levi — build a temple and priesthood outside the Aaronic line and outside Zion (2 Chronicles 6:6), contradicting the Mosaic system; Carroll replies that God *authorized* it (Nephi's vision). See [[Nephite Temple and Priesthood Outside the Levitical Line]].
