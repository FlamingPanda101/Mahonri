# Thoughtful Faith - Is Mother Teresa in Hell

**Channel:** Thoughtful Faith ([[Jacob Hansen]]) · **Type:** Debate review / commentary
**URL:** https://youtu.be/P8qSVI_7pwo

[[Jacob Hansen]] reviews his informal debate with the Australian Reformed evangelist [[Ryan (Protestant Debater)|Ryan Hemlar]] (needgod.net) on [[Brian Davila]]'s show *The Way*, following the logic of Ryan's faith-alone (sola fide) soteriology to what Hansen calls its "uncomfortable conclusion."

- **The reductio:** If trusting Christ *plus* any ordinance (baptism, circumcision) forfeits salvation, then the church fathers, the martyrs, and Catholics like [[Mother Teresa]] — who held baptismal regeneration — are in hell; Ryan estimates only ~10% of ~2 billion professing Christians are truly saved. (The argument is already mapped from the original debate in [[The Church Fathers Reductio Against Sola Fide on Baptism]].)
- **The monergism reveal:** Hansen exposes the Calvinist engine underneath — we only *seem* to choose God; God gives the elect a "new heart" that produces faith, which is itself "an empty hand" of no intrinsic value. See [[Regeneration Precedes Faith So We Only Seem to Choose God]].
- **The moral-monster charge:** Hansen argues the resulting God — who authored the Fall and creates most people for hell to display his justice — is "not worthy of worship," and that this portrait breeds atheists like [[Christopher Hitchens]]. See [[Calvinism Turns God Into a Moral Monster That Breeds Atheism]].
- **The comparative thesis:** Better to err on God's *ontology* (the LDS view) than on God's *character* (Calvinism). See [[Getting Gods Character Wrong Is Worse Than Getting His Ontology Wrong]].
