# In Philosopher's Garb — Is This the Best Argument for Sola Scriptura?

A Protestant philosophy channel's attempt to "raise the bar" on the *sola scriptura* debate by **redefining** the doctrine as a modest methodological principle and defending it with three arguments — the centerpiece being an **argument from epistemic privilege**. The host claims this reformulation lets him "sidestep 80% of" the standard Catholic and Orthodox objections (self-refutation, the canon problem, the "you need the church" gotcha) because his definition never asserts inspiration, infallibility, or a closed canon.[^1] Presented here as a fair, rigorous statement of the strongest Protestant case, engaged by the vault's Catholic/LDS counter-cluster.

- **The redefinition (the whole hook):** *sola scriptura* is not "Scripture is the sole infallible rule" but "when doing theology, give the Bible **veto power** over later church traditions and teachings" — a methodological weighting that is deliberately silent on inspiration, infallibility, and canon.[^2] See [[Sola Scriptura as the Bibles Veto Power Over Tradition]].
- **Argument 1 — epistemic privilege (the flagship):** a detective interviews the eyewitness in the house (Bob), not the man across the world (Grace); likewise the New Testament authors were close to Jesus and the apostles, while a 7th-century council sits ~750 years downstream with no evidence the Holy Spirit secures its theology — so we are justified in giving the earlier sources more weight.[^3] See [[The Epistemic Privilege Argument for Sola Scriptura]].
- **Argument 2 — biblical precedent:** the noble Bereans tested even Paul against the scriptures (Acts 17:11); Paul told the Galatians to reject even an apostle or angel preaching a contrary gospel (Gal 1:8) and publicly rebuked Peter (Gal 2:11-14) — the very "test the new teaching against the prior deposit" method he is defending.[^4] See [[Biblical Precedent for Testing Teaching Against Scripture]].
- **Argument 3 — self-correction / falsifiability:** *sola scriptura* is the church's only means of escaping error; the Catholic/Orthodox model that makes the church the final interpreter is an unfalsifiable circle (the accused running his own investigation), leaving a member with no method to detect that his church is wrong.[^5] See [[Sola Scriptura as the Churchs Only Escape From Error]].
