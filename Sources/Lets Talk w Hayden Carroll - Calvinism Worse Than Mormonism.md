# Lets Talk w Hayden Carroll - Calvinism Worse Than Mormonism

**Channel:** Let's Talk w/ Hayden Carroll | **Host:** [[Hayden Carroll]] (LDS)
**Guests:** [[Tim Stratton]] and [[Josh Klene]] (Freethinking Ministries, Protestant Molinists)
**Duration:** ~99 min

## Summary

- An unusual LDS–Protestant alliance episode: a Latter-day Saint host and two Protestant Molinists agree that **Calvinism** — specifically **exhaustive divine determinism (ED)** — has problems the presenters judge *worse* than the ones Protestants aim at Mormonism. The title comes from a talk Stratton gave with Phil Colberg at the Evangelical Philosophical Society (Boston), applying the "different gospel" test used against Mormonism to Calvinism instead.
- [[Tim Stratton]], a former five-point Calvinist, lays out his **TRUMP** acronym as the Molinist replacement for TULIP and presses his flagship **Deity of Deception Argument** (plus the **Freethinking Argument**) to show that if God determines all beliefs, no theological belief — including Calvinism itself — can be justified.
- [[Josh Klene]] argues Calvinism follows only from idiosyncratic redefinitions of *sovereignty* and *predestination*, and that the confessions' own doctrine of **evanescent grace** means Calvinism cannot ground assurance of salvation.
- The three also probe where LDS and Molinist thought converge (middle knowledge, D&C 137, soul-making) and where they part (the Trinity, and whether getting God's *ontology* wrong is soteriologically disqualifying).

## Concepts Discussed

1. [[TRUMP - The Molinist Alternative to TULIP]] — Stratton's Molinist acronym (Total depravity, Resistible prevenient grace, Unlimited atonement, Middle knowledge, Perseverance of free saints) replacing TULIP.
2. [[The Freethinking Argument - Determinism Undermines Rationality]] — a determined mind cannot rationally infer; the argument Freethinking Ministries is named after.
3. [[The Deity of Deception Argument Against Exhaustive Divine Determinism]] — ED makes God determine everyone to hold false beliefs, so his word cannot be trusted and no theological belief is justified.
4. [[Predestination Does Not Entail Determinism]] — Klene's redefinition steelman and Stratton's argument that predestination ≠ determination.
5. [[Calvinism Is a Different Gospel - GOSPEL vs ED]] — Stratton's GOSPEL acronym inverted point-by-point into the "gospel" of exhaustive divine determinism, then placed under Galatians 1:9's anathema.
6. [[Calvinism Cannot Ground Assurance of Salvation]] — the evanescent-grace problem in the Westminster and London Baptist confessions.
7. [[Getting Gods Character Wrong Is Worse Than Getting His Ontology Wrong]] — the comparative-theology core of the "is Calvinism worse than Mormonism" question.
8. [[Doctrine and Covenants 137 Anticipates Middle Knowledge]] — Joseph Smith's 1836 vision grounds salvation on a counterfactual, which Stratton and Carroll read as middle knowledge.
