# Ward Radio — Luke's Lacuna

A Ward Radio episode hosted by [[Cardon Ellis]] with [[Jonah Barnes]] ("associate professor of all things apocryphal") and [[Austin Falter]], arguing that the resurrected Christ's forty-day ministry is a **measurable gap** between the end of Luke and the beginning of Acts — "Luke's Lacuna" — and that the LDS temple **endowment** is what belongs in the hole. This is the same core thesis as the [[Ward Radio - Secrets of the LDS Temple Endowment Supercut|Temple Endowment Supercut]] cluster and mainly reinforces [[The Lost Forty-Day Teaching Points to a Suppressed Temple Endowment]]; its **distinct** contributions are (1) the word-count "lacuna" and single-author Luke→Acts framing, (2) the [[D&C 95|D&C 95:8-9]] / Joseph-Smith-1843 corroboration, and (3) catacomb-art and excavated-vestment material evidence.

## Summary

- **"Luke's Lacuna": the risen Lord's ministry is a quantifiable hole.** Luke wrote both Luke and Acts (John is "stuck in the middle"); sew the end of Luke to the start of Acts and the seam is the forty days. By Barnes's word counts, Luke gives ~2,000 words to Jesus's pre-ministry, ~17,000 to the mortal ministry, ~6,389 to Holy Week — but only **211 words** to "the Acts of the Risen Lord," then ~24,000 to the apostles. The dip is the lacuna, and the Book of Mormon's **Bountiful ministry** ([[3 Nephi]]) is offered as what fills it. See [[Luke's Lacuna - The Forty-Day Ministry Is a Measurable Hole Between Luke and Acts]]. [^1]
- **Joseph Smith independently filled the hole with the temple endowment.** [[D&C 95|D&C 95:8-9]] commands the Saints to build a house "in the which house I design to endow those whom I have chosen with power from on high… therefore I command you to tarry even as mine apostles at Jerusalem" — reproducing the Luke/Acts pattern before the forty-day apocrypha were known; and Joseph's 1843 discourse says Christ "trained the holy apostles" in "the very same order of things" as his own restored ordinances. See [[Joseph Smith Identified the Temple Endowment With Christ's Teaching to the Apostles at Jerusalem]]. [^2]
- **The disciples were told to be "endued [clothed] with power" and then stayed "continually in the temple."** [[Luke 24.49|Luke 24:49]] ("tarry… until ye be endued with power from on high") uses a Greek clothing verb (*endyō*), and [[Luke 24.50-53|Luke 24:50-53]] has Jesus lift his hands, bless them, and the disciples return to be "continually in the temple." Notably this episode **distinguishes** that clothing language from [[Acts 1.1-5|Acts 1:4]]'s "promise of the Father," where the supercut had blurred them (see the etymology caution below). [^3]
- **The forty-day ritual tradition survives in material, not just textual, form — and was rejected by both sides.** Barnes points to catacomb paintings and excavated garments (ring/prayer circles, altars, changed vestments, crowns, aprons and sashes, washings and anointings, new names, rosettes over the breast, a shoulder knot) as "this is what we're doing" evidence; the forty-day texts (including the first-century **Didache**) were rejected by Protestants as too ritualistic/Catholic and by Catholics as Gnostic. See [[Early Christian Catacomb Art and Vestments Depict Temple-Style Ordinances]]. [^4]

> **Etymology caution (carried from the cluster):** the *endued → endowed* link is only partly sound. The KJV "endued" at [[Luke 24.49|Luke 24:49]] does render a genuine Greek clothing verb (*endyō*, "to put on"), so "clothed with power" is textually fair; but English "endue" (clothe, from Latin *induere*) and "endow" (gift, from *dotare*) are **distinct roots** conflated by sound — so "it's where we get the word endowed" overstates a real image into a false cognate. This episode is more careful than the [[Ward Radio - Secrets of the LDS Temple Endowment Supercut|supercut]], which wrongly tied *Acts 1:4* ("promise" = *epangelia*) to *endo-*. See [[The Lost Forty-Day Teaching Points to a Suppressed Temple Endowment]] §3.
