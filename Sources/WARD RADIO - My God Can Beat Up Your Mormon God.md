# WARD RADIO - My God Can Beat Up Your Mormon God

Ward Radio panel (Cardon Ellis hosting, with Luke Hansen, Jacob Hansen of Thoughtful Faith, and KJ Hershey of 1830 Studios) reacting to [[Brian Holdsworth]]'s video *The Mormon God Problem*, in which Holdsworth stages a "head-to-head stats comparison" between the God of classical/Nicene Christianity (the Trinity) and the LDS Heavenly Father, asking which is "greater" and "more consistent."

- **Holdsworth's argument.** Opening from Anselm's definition of God as "that than which nothing greater can be conceived," Holdsworth argues the classical Trinity — eternal, uncreated, uncaused, unmoved, simple, pure spirit — is by definition greater than an "exalted man," so the LDS God loses the showdown.[^1]
- **The panel's core rebuttal.** Holdsworth's "greater" means ontological **fundamentality** (position in the causal chain), not moral goodness — a Greek/Aristotelian presupposition Latter-day Saints reject. So the comparison is decided by whichever side writes the rulebook, and (as even a Catholic interlocutor conceded) using the ontological argument against people who already reject it is "preaching to the choir."[^2]
- **The worship climax.** The panel's sharpest objection is that an unaffectable, impassible God entails the worshiper has "zero value" to God; they counter that worthiness of worship comes from God's *freely chosen* virtue and his power to save (the *Lectures on Faith* criterion), not from metaphysical ultimacy.[^3]
- **Side notes.** The panel repeatedly criticizes Holdsworth's opening use of imagery from the discredited anti-Mormon film *The God Makers* as a strategic and ethical misstep, and (separately) his stated view that capital punishment of heretics can be justified depending on time and place.[^4]
