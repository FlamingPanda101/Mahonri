# Thoughtful Faith — They Have the Wrong God

**Channel:** Thoughtful Faith ([[Jacob Hansen]])
**Thesis:** The deepest evidence for the Great Apostasy is not the loss of priesthood authority but the *replacement of the biblical God with the God of Greek philosophy* — the God of Abraham quietly became the God of Aristotle.

## Summary
- The loss of apostolic/priesthood authority only *triggered* the apostasy; the apostasy *itself* was the corruption of doctrine that followed — above all the doctrine of God. See [[The Deepest Apostasy Was Trading the God of Abraham for the God of Aristotle]].
- Post-apostolic apologists ([[Justin Martyr]], [[Tertullian]], [[Irenaeus of Lyons|Irenaeus]]), raised in Greco-Roman culture, answered Greek objections by *granting* Greek metaphysics and arguing the biblical God was the same God as the philosophers' — [[Theophilus of Antioch]] describing God with a litany of negations. See [[Apologists Claimed the Biblical God Was the Same God as the Greek Philosophers]].
- **Divine simplicity** (illustrated with a Lego house) is the hidden engine: the most fundamental thing can have no parts, so only one "thing" can be God — which excludes the Father and Son both being God, and forces the Trinity's "three things into one thing" contortions. See [[The Lego Argument - Divine Simplicity Forces a Monotheism That Excludes Distinct Divine Persons]].
- Follow classical theism to its end and "you don't just end up with a different explanation of God — you end up with a different God"; [[Joseph Smith]] restored the personal, relational, embodied God, not merely authority.
