# Ward Radio — How To Destroy EVERY Anti-Mormon Argument

A Ward Radio panel asks two short-form LDS apologists — [[Devin Barnes]] ([[Latterday Logic|Latter-day Logic]]) and a panelist called Raven — for the anti-Mormon arguments they most often field online and how they answer them. The spine of the episode is Barnes's **four-step (abbreviated from fourteen-step) triage framework** for any accusation; the rest works specific arguments (Joseph Smith and Helen Mar Kimball, Adam-God transmission, the Trinity, Isaiah 43:10, Book of Mormon archaeology, Islam, the Book of Abraham) and closes on why people actually leave.

## Summary
- **The framework is the method.** Barnes built a free "Latter-day Logic" tool that runs any accusation through steps — is it *true* (burden on the accuser), is *your* church guilty too, is it *biblical* (and does God condone it), and finally *how does this prove the Book of Mormon false?* — the last step being the keystone move.[^1] See [[The Latter-day Logic Four-Step Framework for Any Anti-Mormon Claim]].
- **Step 1 does most of the work.** Making the accuser carry the burden of proof dissolves most claims: "95% of the time they leave you alone because they either can't find the evidence or they're too lazy to find it."[^2]
- **A reciprocal honesty demand.** The hosts propose that once a specific claim is corrected with primary sources (e.g., "Joseph was just a treasure digger"), the critic is "morally obligated to stop repeating that trope" — even while remaining free to reject the faith.[^3] See [[A Corrected Falsehood Creates a Duty to Stop Repeating It]].
- **The diagnosis of motive.** The panel argues many faith exits are driven by a moral/behavioral issue the person won't repent of, with intellectual objections serving as a "smoke screen" — while conceding it is usually not deliberate.[^4] Corroborates [[Diagnose the Struggle - Moral Disagreement vs Intellectual Doubt]].
