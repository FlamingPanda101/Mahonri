# Thoughtful Faith — The REAL Reason the Church Doesn't Accept Gay Marriage

**Channel:** Thoughtful Faith ([[Jacob Hansen]]) · **URL:** https://youtu.be/JcpZzVPYAVo

A single-thread argument that the whole debate over gay marriage is downstream of one prior question — **"What is gender for?"** — and that every rule about sexuality "floats in midair" until that teleological question is answered.

- **The framing move.** Common Christian answers ("because God said so," "it's unnatural," "it just seems wrong") are *assertions, not explanations* — they never name the purpose being violated. And morality, Hansen argues, is about **the ideal, not merely the permissible**.[^1]
- **Two worldviews that can't answer.** Secular ethics reduces to **consent** (which fixes what is *permitted*, never what marriage/family/gender are *for*); creedal Christianity makes marriage a **mortal institution that ends at death**, which drains gender of eternal significance and leaves the male-female rule looking like **arbitrary command** — with no principled difference between a same-sex couple and an infertile heterosexual couple.[^2]
- **The Restoration's answer.** Gender is "an essential characteristic of individual pre-mortal, mortal, and eternal identity and purpose" (Family Proclamation); marriage and family are eternal; so the commandments are **invitations to an eternal order**, not arbitrary rules or expressions of disgust.[^3]
- **The charitable turn.** Hansen concedes same-sex love can be genuine, faithful, and fulfilling — and that the Church can be *glad* people find companionship — but reframes the question: not *whether love exists* but *whether the relationship embodies the eternal pattern* God is preparing his children for, because "exaltation is not merely about affection… it's about participation in a divine order."[^4]
