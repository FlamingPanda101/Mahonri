# The Stick of Joseph — Book of Mormon Names Authenticate Ancient Origins (Rob Kay)

Returning guest [[Rob Kay]] — a Jewish convert to the Church with a Hebrew (and Greek/Hungarian) background — walks hosts [[Hayden Carroll]] and [[Dave Butler]] through the Hebrew meanings of Book of Mormon proper names. His governing thesis: the 337 proper names (188 unique to the book) are **not invented** but **Hebrew phonetic word-constructs** — an English scribe's best spelling of a Hebrew word the translator sounded out — whose meanings the text plants nearby by the scribal "law of first mention." He layers a Kabbalistic reading (Zohar, sephirot, gematria, *heikhalot*/palaces and *merkavah* literature) on top, which he and the hosts repeatedly flag as speculative.

- **Names as heard-and-transliterated Hebrew.** Because Hebrew letters carry multiple sounds and the scribe spelled a *sound* (not a dictated spelling), "odd" letters (the ambiguous *C* in *curelom*) and the variant spellings of Nahom/NHM on old maps are expected, not evidence of fraud. [^1] [^2] [^3]
- **The palace-and-ark name family.** Mormon (*armon*, citadel/palace), Moroni (*aron*, the ark), and Moronihah (*aron* + *Yah*, "ark of Yah") are read as temple-throne names; Moronihah's Helaman campaign re-enacts the ark's encirclement of Jericho. [^4] [^5] [^6]
- **Jaredite names are Moroni's Hebrew translations.** Coriantumr is decoded as the Hebrew title *Sar-On-Tom-Ur* ("mighty / strength / complete / wisdom"), matching Helaman's own description of him — evidence, Rob argues, that there are "no genuine Jaredite names." [^7] [^8]
- **The ridiculed names resolve to Hebrew roots.** *Curelom* ≈ *shor* (ox), *cumom* ≈ *shamem* (a large beast), *Shez* ≈ *shetef* (sweeping flood), *Lachoneus* (Greek-looking but Semitic, "cross over / flee"), *Abinadi* ("father of my exile"), *Nehor* (*nahar*, river-conduit) — each glossed to fit its bearer's narrative role. [^9] [^10] Co-host [[Dave Butler]] adds that two Alma passages show every trait of a Urim-and-Thummim military consultation "but the name." [^11]
