# Atheism Has a History Problem...

Alex Arnold of *Moroni's Standard* — a former atheist who "read Dawkins and applied the same standards" — turns Carl Sagan's maxim "extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence" back on philosophical materialism itself.[^1] His thesis is methodological, not theological: apply the *same* evidentiary standard atheists use on religious claims to "the actual physics, the actual archaeology, and the actual philosophy of mind," and materialism "fails it across every field simultaneously."[^2] Every witness he calls — Penrose, Hoyle, Chalmers, Nagel, Crick, Flew — is a secular or atheist scholar who "just followed their own fields to conclusions that ended up complicating their own positions."[^3] The argument does not claim to prove God, only that "a designing intelligence behind the cosmos" is "the more coherent position given what we actually have."[^4]

- **The burden is symmetric.** Materialism is a positive claim (matter/energy under natural law is "the complete and sufficient explanation for everything"), so "definitional retreat" (redefining atheism as mere lack of belief) and Russell's Teapot are burden-shifting dodges, not a free pass.[^5]
- **Four fields, four failures.** Cosmic fine-tuning (Penrose's low-entropy initial state, Hoyle's carbon resonance), the hard problem of consciousness (Chalmers, Nagel), the origin-of-life information problem (Crick, Flew), and the archaeological record of religion (Göbekli Tepe, Cauvin) each defeat the confident materialist default.[^2]
- **The multiverse, abiogenesis, and reducible consciousness are themselves faith claims.** Each is "unfalsifiable, unobservable, and requiring belief in entities beyond detection" — the very standard atheists reject in theism.[^6]
- **Both positions require faith, so the question shifts.** With "no neutral ground," the real question is "which account does the available evidence actually support," and Berlinski's *Devil's Delusion* charges that new-atheist confidence "vastly exceeds its evidential warrant."[^7]
