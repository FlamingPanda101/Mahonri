# Lets Talk w Hayden Carroll - Rethinking Same-Sex Attraction

**Channel:** Let's Talk w/ Hayden Carroll | **Guests:** [[Jeff Robinson]] (marriage & family therapist), [[Andrew Robinson]] (religious educator, his son), [[Timber Harvard|Timber Harward]] (faithful LDS convert who experiences SSA)
**Format:** Unscripted Pride-Month panel (~56 min) | **Tradition:** [[Hayden Carroll|LDS]]

## Summary

- The organizing thesis, from [[Jeff Robinson]]'s ~30 years of clinical work, is that
  same-sex attraction is best understood not as an innate identity but as
  **"something you know how to do"** — a reframe that, he argues, restores moral agency
  where "born that way" removes it. He surveys five folk-explanations of cause and grants
  each partial truth. See [[Same-Sex Attraction Is Something a Person Learns to Do Not an Innate Identity]].
- Much of the conversation is a sustained **assumption-audit**: the panel argues the
  "born gay" premise is unsupported by both science and LDS prophets, and that adopting
  the totalizing **"gay" label** turns ordinary traits into "evidence" of a fixed category
  and becomes a core obstacle to discipleship. See
  [[The Born-Gay Assumption Is Unsupported by Science and LDS Prophets]] and
  [[Adopting the Gay Label Hinders Discipleship]].
- Robinson offers a clinical **developmental account** (three predisposing traits; two
  onset patterns; five factors that "lock it in" via overfocus) and a therapeutic lever —
  a feeling with **no sexual content is not homosexual**, so ceasing to over-interpret
  reduces the distress. [[Timber Harvard|Timber Harward]] corroborates from experience that
  the attraction's *salience* fell without the attraction itself simply vanishing.
- The pastoral core: affirmation "**relieves shame** but strands the disciple" on the
  unanswered question *how do I then live a covenant life?* — so **pride is not the
  antidote to shame; humility is** — and the first move with anyone who discloses is to
  listen, not correct. The topic is sensitive; the panel members repeatedly disclaim any
  intent to shame those who experience SSA.

## Concepts Discussed

1. [[Same-Sex Attraction Is Something a Person Learns to Do Not an Innate Identity]] — the "something you know how to do" reframe; five approaches to cause; agency vs. innateness.
2. [[The Born-Gay Assumption Is Unsupported by Science and LDS Prophets]] — no clear genetic determinant; prophets have not taught it; "good-faith reason" and "I don't know" both smuggle the assumption.
3. [[Adopting the Gay Label Hinders Discipleship]] — label-as-identity vs. adjective; the gravity analogy (naming ≠ explaining); President Nelson and President Oaks on labels.
4. [[A Feeling With No Sexual Content Is Not Homosexual]] — separating platonic male bonding and unmet needs from homosexuality; over-interpretation as the multiplier.
5. [[Robinson's Developmental Account of Same-Sex Attraction]] — three predisposing traits; the "overly chaste" and "felt different / rejected" onset patterns.
6. [[Overfocus and Overinterpretation Entrench Same-Sex Attraction]] — the five factors that lock it in; what you focus on grows; de-focusing lowers salience.
7. [[Pride Is Not the Antidote to Shame]] — affirmation relieves shame but leaves "how do I live a life?" unanswered; humility and charity as the durable antidote.
8. [[Listen Before Correcting When Someone Discloses Same-Sex Attraction]] — the pastoral method: validate and hear first; gently question assumptions only after a relationship exists.
