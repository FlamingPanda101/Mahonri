# Protestant vs Mormon: Who Goes To Heaven?

On his channel *The Way*, [[Brian Davila]] hosts a long, heated debate on the question "how can we be saved?" between [[Ryan (Protestant Debater)|Ryan]], a Reformed Protestant street/online evangelist from Australia, and Latter-day Saint apologist [[Jacob Hansen]] (Thoughtful Faith).[^1] Ryan's thesis: salvation is *sola fide* — entry into heaven rests "purely on the merits of Christ received by faith," works are the *fruit* of a living faith and never a *condition* of it, and the believer is eternally kept. Hansen's thesis: faith in Christ is how we enter a *covenant relationship*, but works are "necessary but not sufficient" — one is finally judged by works to determine which degree of glory ("being with Heavenly Father") one inherits.

## Participants
- [[Ryan (Protestant Debater)|Ryan]] (Reformed Protestant evangelist) vs. [[Jacob Hansen]] (Latter-day Saint apologist, Thoughtful Faith), moderated by host [[Brian Davila]].

## Summary
- **Grace, faith, and the "necessary but not sufficient" wedge.** Ryan opens by steelmanning and reading Ephesians 2:8-9 and Romans 4:4-5 (gift vs. wage); Hansen agrees grace saves but insists works are "necessary but not sufficient" and offers a syllogism (works → living faith → salvation). Ryan replies faith is *both* necessary *and* sufficient, and any added law-keeping "severs" one from Christ (Galatians 5). See [[Works Are Necessary but Not Sufficient vs Faith Is Necessary and Sufficient]].[^2]
- **What "justification" and "heaven" even mean.** Hansen deploys the New Perspective on Paul (justification = present covenant membership, not a going-to-heaven verdict; citing N. T. Wright) and reads Romans 5:9's "much more shall we be saved" as provisional on covenant faithfulness at the final judgment; Ryan reads it as a guarantee and Philippians 3:9 as imputed righteousness. See [[Justification Is Covenant Entry Not a Guaranteed Final Verdict]].[^3]
- **Degrees of glory vs. one heaven; the church-fathers reductio.** They dispute whether the lowest kingdom is still "heaven" (D&C 76's differing divine visitation, a Papias 100/60/30-fold quote) versus being with the triune God (John 14's "many mansions"); and Hansen argues that if baptism is necessary, the church fathers and martyrs are damned, which Ryan meets with Matthew 7 and the claim most fathers were silent. See [[Being With Heavenly Father vs a Reward in Glory]] and [[The Church Fathers Reductio Against Sola Fide on Baptism]].[^4]
- **Assurance, perseverance, and the Kimball/McConkie tension.** Ryan claims 100% assurance (kept by God; Romans 8, John 10, Jude 1); Hansen says he cannot be certain because he is a free agent who could reject Christ, appealing to John 15 (abiding branches), Hebrews 10:26, and 1 Corinthians 9:27. Ryan reads Spencer W. Kimball ("saved by grace alone... is a doctrine of Satan") against Bruce R. McConkie ("we are saved by grace without works") to argue LDS teaching is internally at odds. See [[Can a True Believer Fall Away]] and [[Kimball vs McConkie - Does LDS Grace Teaching Contradict Itself]].[^5]
