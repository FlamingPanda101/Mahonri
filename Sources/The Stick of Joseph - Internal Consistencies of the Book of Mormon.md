# The Stick of Joseph - Internal Consistencies of the Book of Mormon

Host [[Hayden Carroll]] and co-host Jackson interview guest [[Jeff Markham]] — a FAIR writer-contributor — who argues that the [[Book of Mormon]] is laced with roughly 120 "internal consistencies": subtle factual details planted in one place and only redeemed dozens of chapters (weeks of dictation) later, with no contradictions. Because [[Joseph Smith]] dictated the ~270,000-word text in ~60 working days with his face in a hat, no notes, no manuscript, and no revisions, Markham argues the sheer bookkeeping load of these undesigned coincidences is unaccountable on any naturalistic "literary genius" or "automatic writing" theory. He walks through worked examples (a timeline, a geography, a characterization, a re-told conversion, a 400-year prophecy, and the 30 reversed Jaredite kings) and stresses throughout that the "party trick" is a gateway to devotional study, not the point of the book.

## Summary

- **The core claim.** ~120–122 internally consistent details span verses, chapters, whole books, and sometimes multiple books; the rarer and farther-apart the pair, the more impressive. The signature case is Alma 27:17's "he fell **again** to the earth," which only resolves ~20 chapters earlier in Mosiah 27:12 [00:00], [03:01], [07:00], [17:30].
- **The dictation constraint amplifies the difficulty.** Markham (citing [[Brian Hales]]) frames the text as one ~270,000-word "verbatim run-on sentence" with no edits or corrections, dictated by an unschooled farm boy (Oliver Cowdery met Joseph 5 Apr, translation began 7 Apr, finished end of June). A slip could not be scratched out and fixed later [16:00], [28:00].
- **Worked examples.** A Mosiah reign-timeline that arithmetically closes (6:4 ↔ 29:46); King Limhi's guards characterization (Mosiah 7:10 ↔ 21:19); Zarahemla-and-Gideon geography 56 chapters apart (Alma 6:7 ↔ 61:5); Alma's conversion re-told to his son with ten details in order and audience-tailored variation (Mosiah 27 ↔ Alma 36); Alma's secret 400-year prophecy fulfilled in Mormon 8:6; and the 30 Jaredite kings listed in reverse then narrated forward in exact order [18:00]–[52:00].
- **The dilemma Markham presses.** Critics who concede the text is too intricate for Joseph either credit a superhuman literary genius (contradicted by his meagre later output and the View-of-the-Hebrews / Spalding non-parallels) or retreat to demonic possession — either way conceding the supernatural. Markham's counter: a book that moves millions to forsake sin and come to Christ is not plausibly Satan's work [40:30], [45:01], [52:30].
