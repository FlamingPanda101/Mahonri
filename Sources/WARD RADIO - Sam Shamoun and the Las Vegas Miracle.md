# Ward Radio — Sam Shamoun and the Las Vegas Miracle

- **Central story:** Two LDS creators — [[Latterday Logic]] (Devin) and [[Gospel Guardian]] (Braden) — debated the notoriously combative anti-Mormon polemicist [[Sam Shamoun]] on Brian Davila's channel "The Way," in Las Vegas. Instead of the expected "UFC blood bath," Shamoun publicly said he *lost the debate because his heart was changed*, "fell in love" with the two men, and now questions whether he wants to debate at all. The panel frames this as the "Las Vegas Miracle." [^1] [^2]
- **The method claim:** The guests attribute the turn not to argument but to a deliberate, prayed-for posture of Christlike love — they fasted, gifted Shamoun a Book of Mormon, opened in prayer, and dropped their prepared "gotcha" traps mid-debate on spiritual promptings — offered as "exhibit A" that debate can witness Christ's love, not just score points. See [[Christlike Love Softened a Hostile Debater - The Las Vegas Miracle]]. [^3] [^4]
- **Hostile-witness concessions:** On camera Shamoun (a) defined the Trinity via the **monarchy of the Father** and granted it "sounds super similar to what we believe in terms of the godhead," conceding Western Trinitarianism departs from the church fathers [^5]; (b) admitted the **LDS vision of heaven/exaltation is more compelling** than the Protestant "celibate bachelor" heaven and retracted a caricature he'd been spreading [^6]; and (c) leaned on the apocryphal **2 Maccabees 7:28** for creation *ex nihilo* — which the guests answer with **Wisdom 11:17** and the "formed" (Hebrew *yatsar*) argument. [^7]
- **Note on tone:** ~half the 90-minute stream is banter, super-chats, sponsor reads, and the host's chemotherapy update; the theology is concentrated in the clip breakdowns. The panel itself steelmans caution — "let's not start giving him human-being status yet... let's see two or three well-behaved debates" — treating one changed heart as encouraging but not a conversion. [^8]
