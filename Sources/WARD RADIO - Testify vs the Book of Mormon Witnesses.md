# WARD RADIO - Testify vs the Book of Mormon Witnesses

Ward Radio (Cardon Ellis and co-hosts) reacts to a video by the evangelical channel **[[Testify Apologetics]]** (~228–280k subscribers) that tries to debunk **[[Alex OConnor|Alex O'Connor]]**'s argument — raised in O'Connor's [[Trent Horn]] / Jubilee appearances — that consistency should make a Christian take the Book of Mormon witnesses at least as seriously as the resurrection witnesses.

- They rebut Testify's specific charges one by one: that the Three Witnesses' testimony is "super thin"; that the formal statement gives "no day, time, location"; that the plates were "seen by faith" (Doctrine and Covenants 17) yet also physically hidden from thieves (the "barrel of beans" problem); and that granting the witnesses' sincerity still leaves the case weak.
- Core apologetic moves: **faith = divine authorization to view a real physical object** (tabernacle / Holy of Holies and [[The Nephite Sacred Cache Parallels the Ark of the Covenant|Ark of the Covenant]] parallels); **granting the witnesses' sincerity actually collapses Testify's own "missing details" objection**; and demanding ever-more circumstantial evidence is a **goalpost-moving double standard** (the "we know where Jerusalem is, not Zarahemla" juror analogy).
- The episode endorses O'Connor's "reverse pull" point: because all three witnesses were *excommunicated* yet never denied their testimony, they satisfy the criterion of embarrassment — making their witness, per O'Connor, *stronger* than the "why would a persecutor lie?" case for Paul.
- Tone is mocking and comedic ("the most hilarious enemy of the church I have ever seen"), so weigh the substance over the presentation; authority **C**.
