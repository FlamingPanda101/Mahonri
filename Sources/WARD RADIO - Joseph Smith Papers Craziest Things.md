# WARD RADIO - Joseph Smith Papers Craziest Things

Ward Radio interviews [[Ryan Wood]], "the Amateur Historian," whose channel narrates the *entire* Joseph Smith Papers. He names his "top five craziest" takeaways from reading all of it, then turns to why he still holds a temple recommend.

- **Who he is.** Wood claims to be one of the very few people — perhaps besides the JSP editors themselves — to have read the whole Joseph Smith Papers, a project that took ~15 years as the volumes appeared roughly every six months through COVID; he has also read the 26-volume Journal of Discourses. [00:30], [01:30], [07:30] He is candid that the corpus is "raw data," repetitive, and often only fully available on the JSP website. [08:00]
- **The "craziest" items.** (1) [[Oliver Cowdery]] was excommunicated in part for **counterfeiting** money after the Kirtland Safety Society failed. [01:30] (2) The Independence, Missouri city plat laid out roughly **24 single-purpose temples**, leading Wood to conclude the Kirtland "temple" was really the church's first **chapel** — the Saints called it the "House of the Lord." [04:30] (3) A **magical/folk worldview** (seer stones, [[Oliver Cowdery]]'s divining rod) was religiously normal, not "evil," in Joseph's era — a D&C revelation text about the rod was later softened. [09:30] (4) The **Kirtland Temple dedication** produced multiply-attested Pentecostal phenomena — angels, fire, a rushing wind, tongues, a song in the "language of Adam." [13:31] (5) Candidly human episodes, e.g. Joseph dictating a **revelation, crumpling it, and throwing it** at someone in anger. [21:00]
- **Affirmative apologetics.** The church does **not** hide its history — the JSP are free online (more than the print volumes), the church owns and gives away *View of the Hebrews*, and critics research inside the Church History Library. [17:31] And the **restraint** of Joseph Smith and Brigham Young — building checks on their own power, and declining revenge after the martyrdom — is, for Wood, an "intellectual testimony." [42:30]
- **Caveats.** Non-credentialed podcast commentary; several legal/biographical specifics are recalled loosely, and the tone is deliberately provocative ("grow up about it"). Sponsor: Go and Do Travel.
