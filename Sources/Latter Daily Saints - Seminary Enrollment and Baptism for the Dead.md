# Latter Daily Saints — Seminary Enrollment and Baptism for the Dead

Episode 186 of *Latter Daily Saints*, hosted by Jackson Wayne Paul with Hayden Ball and guest [[Jonah Barnes]]. It opens on a newly released Church statistic about non-member enrollment in seminary and institute, then pivots to an extended defense of **baptism for the dead** and **postmortem missionary work** against creedal-Christian objections.

## Summary
- **The statistic (the headline):** the Church newsroom reports worldwide seminary-and-institute enrollment now exceeds one million students, **including 85,695 "friends of other faiths" (non-members), a figure that has nearly doubled in two years**, credited to the *Invite a Friend* initiative launched April 2025 under [[Dallin H. Oaks]]. See [[Non-Member Seminary and Institute Enrollment Has Nearly Doubled]].[^1][^2]
- **Baptism for the dead (the core apologetic):** the hosts run [[1 Corinthians 15.29|1 Corinthians 15:29]], [[1 Peter 3.18-20|1 Peter 3:18-20]]/[[1 Peter 3.21|3:21]], Acts 2:31, and Alma 40 / D&C 138 as verses "creedal Christianity has no way of reasoning with," and pile on patristic and apocryphal precedent (John Chrysostom chiding Byzantines for baptizing *and* giving the Eucharist to the dead; the Council of Carthage 397; the Ethiopic Enoch and *Epistula Apostolorum*; 2 Maccabees' prayers for dead soldiers; the Community of Christ expunging the relevant D&C sections in 1990). This is already documented at higher authority in the vault.
- **The predestination dilemma:** Romans 9:13 ("Jacob have I loved, but Esau have I hated") + [[Augustine]]'s *massa damnata* ("damnable blob," 99% of humanity created to burn) is presented as the corner creedal Christianity is forced into once it affirms baptism-required-for-salvation but denies a postmortem chance — the corner the Restoration escapes. See [[Augustine Set Death as the Boundary of Salvation in the West]] and [[Every Person Gets a Fair Opportunity Through Proxy Ordinances]].
- **Postmortem missionary work (Barnes's thesis):** from his book *The Missing Ministry of the Risen Christ*, Barnes argues Christ used the 40 days to commission the apostles to die so they could preach in the spirit world; the martyred apostle [[David W. Patten]] (Battle of Crooked River, 1838) and the English convert [[Anne Booth]]'s vision of him teaching the dead are offered as a Restoration echo. See [[The Apostles Were Martyred to Become Missionaries in the Spirit World]].[^3][^4]
